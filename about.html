Our Story

BigToolz was born out of frustration. In 2024, our founder was working on a small project and needed a simple image compressor. What he found instead was a maze of websites asking for email signups, hiding features behind paywalls, or bombarding him with pop-up ads. A five-second task took fifteen minutes.

That day, the idea for BigToolz was born: a single place where anyone students, developers, designers, small business owners, or just someone in a hurry could find fast, free, and honest tools without the nonsense.

We started with 5 tools. Today, we have over 300. And we're just getting started.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make the web simpler, one tool at a time.

Every tool on BigToolz is built with three principles in mind speed, privacy, and simplicity. We don't ask for your email. We don't store your files on our servers. We don't hide features behind paywalls. If a tool says "free," it means free forever.

We believe the internet should work for you, not the other way around.

Why People Trust BigToolz

Trust isn't given it's earned. Here's how we earn yours every single day:

🔐 Your Files Stay on Your Device

Most of our tools run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. That means when you compress an image or convert a PDF, the file never leaves your computer. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored. Your privacy is absolute.

⚡ No Hidden Costs

Every tool listed as "free" is truly free. We don't lure you in and then ask for payment on the last step. Our revenue comes from ads shown on the page never from your wallet.

🚫 No Signups, No Spam

We don't ask for your email, phone number, or social login. Open the tool, use it, close the tab. That's it.

🛠 Built by Users, for Users

Every tool on BigToolz was built because someone asked for it. We listen to our users and add tools based on real feedback not just what looks good on a marketing slide.
