## ⚠️ Reality Check (Read First)

**No one can guarantee AdSense approval.** Not us, not anyone. Google's review is automated + manual, and policies change constantly.

**What we CAN do:**
- Follow every policy exactly
- Build a high-quality site Google loves
- Avoid every known rejection trigger
- Maximize approval chances to **~95–99%**

**What we CANNOT do:**
- Guarantee approval 100%
- Bypass Google's review
- Speed up review time

**Timeline:**
- **Application:** 1–3 days to submit
- **Initial review:** 24 hours – 2 weeks
- **Full approval:** 2–4 weeks typical
- **Rejection + fix + reapply:** +2 weeks

## 1. Eligibility Requirements

### You Must:

- [ ] **Be 18+ years old** (or have parent/guardian consent)
- [ ] **Own the site** — you must have full control
- [ ] **Live in an eligible country** — most countries qualify
- [ ] **Have a valid Google account** — Gmail works
- [ ] **Have a payment address** — for receiving payments
- [ ] **Have a bank account** — for direct deposit (or check)

### You Must NOT:

- [ ] **Use a VPN** to fake your country
- [ ] **Own a site with prohibited content** — see policy
- [ ] **Have a previously banned AdSense account** — new account won't help
- [ ] **Operate from a sanctioned country** — Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, etc.

### Site Must Be:

- [ ] **Live** — not in development
- [ ] **Accessible** — no password protection or "coming soon"
- [ ] **Have real content** — not a template or placeholder
- [ ] **Own your domain** — not a free subdomain (e.g., `yoursite.blogspot.com` is okay-ish, but not recommended)

## 2. Pre-Application Checklist

### Content

- [ ] **30+ unique, quality pages** — tool pages + blog posts
- [ ] **1,500+ words per tool page** — 2,000+ for blog posts
- [ ] **No AI-only content** — human-written or heavily edited
- [ ] **No plagiarism** — 100% original
- [ ] **No thin content** — every page adds value
- [ ] **Proper grammar** — no obvious errors
- [ ] **Readable** — short paragraphs, bullet lists, subheadings
- [ ] **Updated recently** — "Last updated" date on content

### Required Pages

- [ ] **About Us** — story, mission, team, values
- [ ] **Contact Us** — email, form, response time
- [ ] **Privacy Policy** — cookies, analytics, ads disclosure
- [ ] **Terms of Service** — usage rules, IP, liability
- [ ] **Disclaimer** — limitations, external links, ads
- [ ] **Cookie Policy** — GDPR (part of privacy or separate)
- [ ] **DMCA / Copyright** — takedown process
- [ ] **Sitemap** — HTML + XML

### Technical

- [ ] **HTTPS** — SSL certificate valid
- [ ] **Mobile-friendly** — responsive design
- [ ] **Fast loading** — under 3 seconds
- [ ] **No broken links** — run Screaming Frog
- [ ] **Clean navigation** — header, footer, breadcrumbs
- [ ] **Working search** — site-wide search
- [ ] **404 page** — helpful, not blank
- [ ] **Domain age** — 2–4 weeks minimum (longer is better)
- [ ] **No auto-play video/audio** — annoying
- [ ] **No popups** — avoid intrusive interstitials

### AdSense-Specific

- [ ] **`ads.txt` file** — at root with publisher ID
- [ ] **AdSense code in `<head>`** — after verification
- [ ] **No previous AdSense account issues**
- [ ] **AdSense account matches site owner**

## 3. Content Requirements

### Minimum Content Volume

| Type | Minimum | Recommended | Ideal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tool pages | 20 | 30+ | 50+ |
| Blog posts | 10 | 20+ | 30+ |
| Total pages | 30 | 50+ | 100+ |
| Total word count | 50,000 | 100,000 | 200,000+ |

**BigToolz advantage:** 300 tool pages = **450,000+ words** of unique content. This is way above the minimum — a huge plus for approval.

### Content Quality Rules

**Must Have:**
- ✅ Original content — write it yourself or use AI as a draft
- ✅ Real value — solves problems, answers questions
- ✅ Deep topics — not surface-level
- ✅ Proper sourcing — link to authoritative sites
- ✅ Regular updates — at least quarterly
- ✅ Human touch — experience, examples, opinions

**Must Avoid:**
- ❌ **Copied content** — plagiarism = instant rejection
- ❌ **AI-generated without editing** — Google detects this
- ❌ **Thin content** — under 300 words
- ❌ **Duplicate content** — same content on multiple pages
- ❌ **Keyword stuffing** — over 3% density
- ❌ **Misleading titles** — clickbait
- ❌ **Hidden text** — white-on-white, tiny fonts
- ❌ **Auto-generated content** — scrapers, spinners

### Content You Should NEVER Publish

- ❌ **Adult content** — porn, sexual, suggestive
- ❌ **Violence** — graphic, gore, cruelty
- ❌ **Hate speech** — discriminatory content
- ❌ **Illegal content** — drugs, weapons, hacking
- ❌ **Copyright content** — pirated books, movies, music
- ❌ **Phishing/scams** — fake forms, misleading links
- ❌ **Misinformation** — false medical, political claims
- ❌ **Counterfeit goods** — fake brand names

- What data you collect

- Last updated date

**Length:** 1,500+ words

**⚠️ Critical for AdSense:** Must mention that Google and third-party vendors use cookies to serve ads based on user's prior visits.

## 5. Site Quality Standards

- [ ] **Easy-to-find content** — search + categories
- [ ] **Mobile-friendly** — responsive everywhere
- [ ] **Clear CTAs** — obvious buttons
- [ ] **No auto-redirects** — annoy users
- [ ] **No auto-play audio/video** — annoying

### Trust Signals

- [ ] **Real content** — not just ads or affiliate links
- [ ] **Author bylines** — real names + bios
- [ ] **Contact info** — email, form
- [ ] **Physical address** — optional but builds trust
- [ ] **Business registration** — if applicable
