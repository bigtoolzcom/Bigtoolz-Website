🎯 What is a Keyword Planner?

A Keyword Planner is a research tool that generates keyword ideas from a seed topic and shows estimated metrics for each one search volume, difficulty, CPC, and search intent. It's the first step in any SEO content strategy: you can't rank for keywords you haven't discovered.

Our tool runs 100% in your browser no signup, no usage limits, and no data sent to any server. It generates keyword ideas using proven patterns (long-tail variations, questions, comparisons, commercial modifiers) and assigns metrics based on linguistic and behavioral heuristics.

📊 Keyword Metrics Explained

Search Volume

Estimated monthly searches in the selected country. Higher is better for reach but usually correlates with higher competition. Our estimates are heuristic-based ideal for relative comparison between keywords, not for exact traffic prediction. For precise numbers, use Google Keyword Planner.

Keyword Difficulty (0-100)

An estimate of how hard it is to rank on page 1 for that keyword:

  • 0-20: Easy great for new sites
  • 21-40: Medium reachable with good content
  • 41-60: Hard needs authority + backlinks
  • 61-80: Very hard competitive niches
  • 81-100: Extremely hard top-3 are giants

CPC (Cost Per Click)

Estimated cost an advertiser pays per click in Google Ads. Higher CPC = higher commercial value for that keyword. Keywords with CPC above $5 are usually in finance, insurance, legal, or B2B niches. Useful for prioritising keywords you'd also pay for.

Search Intent

The likely intention behind the search. Matching your content to intent is the single biggest factor in ranking:

💡 Best Practices

  • Start with 3-5 seed keywords. Run each, export the CSVs, then merge.
  • Prioritise long-tail (4+ words). Lower difficulty, higher conversion, faster ranking.
  • Match content to intent. Informational → guide. Commercial → comparison. Transactional → product page.
  • Group keywords by topic. One page can target 10-30 related keywords not one page per keyword.
  • Watch CPC as a value signal. High-CPC keywords are worth more to advertisers and to you.
  • Refresh your list quarterly. Trends shift; what ranked last year may not rank next year.
  • Combine with Search Console data. After publishing, see which keywords you already rank for then optimise.
