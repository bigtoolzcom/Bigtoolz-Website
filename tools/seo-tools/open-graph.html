📘 What is Open Graph?

Open Graph (OG) is a protocol created by Facebook in 2010 that controls how web pages appear when shared on social media. It uses HTML meta tags prefixed with og: to define the title, description, image, and other details shown in link previews.

Without OG tags, social platforms scrape whatever they can find usually the page title and a random image. With proper OG tags, you control exactly how your content appears, dramatically improving click-through rates when shared.

🖼 Ideal Image Sizes

  • Facebook / LinkedIn / WhatsApp: 1200 × 630 px (1.91:1)
  • Twitter summary_large_image: 1200 × 628 px
  • Twitter summary (square): 400 × 400 px
  • Minimum: 600 × 315 px
  • Format: PNG or JPG, under 1MB
  • Safe zone: Keep important content in center 80%

🎯 OG vs Twitter Card

Open Graph is used by Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Slack, Discord, Pinterest, and most platforms. Twitter Card is specific to X/Twitter. Twitter falls back to OG tags if Twitter-specific tags are missing, but including both gives the best preview quality.
