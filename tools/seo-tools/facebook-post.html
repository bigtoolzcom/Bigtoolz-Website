🎯 The 8 Facebook Post Types Explained

📖 Story Posts

Short personal narratives that build connection. "Yesterday, a customer told me..." Story posts create emotional engagement and make your brand human. Facebook's algorithm favors them because they drive comments and shares.

❓ Question Posts

Direct questions to your audience. "What's your biggest challenge with X?" Questions get the highest comment rate of any post type. Facebook loves comments they signal a healthy community and boost your post's reach.

💡 Tip Posts

Quick, actionable advice. "3 ways to improve your X today..." Tips are highly shareable and establish expertise. They also work as lead magnets offer a longer resource in the comments.

🎯 Promo Posts

Product/service promotions. Facebook reduces reach on pure promotion, so wrap it in value. "Our new X solves Y problem. Here's how it works..." Keep promo posts to 20% of your content.

📢 Announcement Posts

News about launches, events, milestones. Announcements build anticipation and give followers a reason to return. Ideal for new products, features, or upcoming webinars.

💬 Quote Posts

Inspirational or thoughtful quotes with your commentary. Easy to create, share, and engage with. Best for motivational brands, personal profiles, and lifestyle pages.

📋 Listicle Posts

"5 ways to...", "7 tools that...", "10 things every X should know." Listicles are scannable, shareable, and work for any niche. Great for saving as content references.

🎬 Behind-the-Scenes Posts

Show the process behind your work. "Here's how we make X" or "A day in the life of..." BTS content builds authenticity and loyalty. Best for creative, manufacturing, and service businesses.

💡 Facebook Post Best Practices

  • First 80 characters matter most. Posts under 80 chars get 66% more engagement. Hook fast.
  • Use 1-2 images per post. Posts with images get 2.3x more engagement than text-only.
  • Ask questions. Question posts get 3-5x more comments than statements.
  • Post when your audience is online. Check Facebook Insights for peak times.
  • Reply within the first hour. Early engagement tells Facebook your post is valuable.
  • Limit hashtags to 1-3. Facebook isn't Instagram hashtags have minimal impact.
  • Use emojis strategically. 1-3 emojis per post boost engagement 20-30%. More looks spammy.
  • Mix content types. Aim for 80% value, 20% promotion over-promotion kills reach.
  • Test posting times. Business pages: 8 AM, 12 PM, 5 PM typically perform well.
  • Videos autoplay. Posts with video get 2x more engagement than static images.
