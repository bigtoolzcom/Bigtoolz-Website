▦ What is a QR Code with Logo?

A QR code with logo (also called a "branded QR code") is a QR code that has a small logo or image placed in the center. It looks more professional and on-brand than a plain black-and-white QR code, and typically increases scan rates because users instantly recognize the brand behind it. From restaurant menus to product packaging to business cards branded QR codes are everywhere.

Our generator creates QR codes with your logo embedded in the center 100% in your browser. No uploads, no server, no watermark. Download as PNG (with logo) or SVG (vector) for print or web.

📐 How QR Codes with Logo Work

QR codes contain error correction data extra bits that allow scanners to recover missing or damaged portions. There are 4 levels:

  • L (Low): 7% recovery safest QR code size, smallest file.
  • M (Medium): 15% recovery balanced. Default for many QR codes.
  • Q (Quartile): 25% recovery for codes that may get damaged.
  • H (High): 30% recovery required for logos. Allows up to 30% of the QR code to be covered.

When you embed a logo, it covers part of the QR code. As long as the coverage stays under the error correction threshold (30% for level H), the QR code remains perfectly scannable. Our tool uses level H by default and keeps the logo under 22% by default well within safe limits.

💡 Best Practices for QR Codes with Logo

  • Use error correction H. Always use level H (30% recovery) when embedding a logo.
  • Keep logo small. 15-22% of the QR code area. Bigger looks cleaner but hurts scannability.
  • Use PNG with transparency. A logo with a white/colored background creates a visible box a transparent PNG blends seamlessly.
  • Square logos work best. If your logo is wide/tall, crop it or place it in a square container.
  • Test-scan before printing. Scan with 3-4 different apps on different phones. If one fails, reduce logo size or switch to level Q.
  • Add a white border around the logo. This prevents the logo from blending into dark QR modules.
  • Keep colors high-contrast. Dark QR on light background always scans best.
  • Quiet zone matters. Leave a 4-module margin around the QR code otherwise scanners may fail.
