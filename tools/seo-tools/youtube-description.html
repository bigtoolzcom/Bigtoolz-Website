🎯 YouTube Description Structure (Anatomy)

1. Hook (First 100-150 Characters)

This is the most important part. Only the first ~150 characters show above the "Show More" button. Include your primary keyword here and make it compelling. This section also appears in Google search results when your video is indexed.

2. Detailed Description (150-300 words)

Explain what the video covers in more detail. Include secondary keywords naturally. This helps YouTube and Google understand the video's topic and rank it for more searches. Use paragraphs not one giant block of text.

3. Timestamps / Chapters

Chapters let viewers jump to specific parts of your video. YouTube requires: minimum 3 chapters, each at least 10 seconds, starting with 0:00. Chapters create clickable navigation, improving watch time and user experience. They also appear in Google search.

4. Links (Resources, Social, Affiliate)

Add links after the description: your website, social profiles, related videos, free downloads, affiliate products. Each link must be on its own line to be clickable. Put the most important link first.

5. Call-to-Action (CTA)

Ask viewers to like, comment, subscribe, or click a link. A clear CTA increases engagement metrics which YouTube uses as a ranking signal. Don't be shy ask for the subscribe.

6. Hashtags (3-5 Max)

Hashtags help with topic discovery. YouTube shows the first 3 above your video title. Use 3-5 max over 15 causes YouTube to ignore all of them. Pick tags relevant to your niche.

💡 YouTube Description Best Practices

  • Front-load keywords. The first 1-2 sentences carry the most SEO weight.
  • Aim for 200-300 words. Enough to rank, not so long that it becomes spam.
  • Include your main keyword 2-3 times in the description not more, or it looks spammy.
  • Add chapters for videos over 5 minutes. Boosts watch time and Google visibility.
  • Put important links at the top. Above the "Show More" cut for maximum clicks.
  • Use line breaks. Dense walls of text get skipped. Break into short paragraphs.
  • Include a CTA. Ask viewers to subscribe, comment, or click a link.
  • Use 3-5 hashtags. More than 15 = YouTube ignores all.
  • Update descriptions. Add new links or fix outdated info periodically.
