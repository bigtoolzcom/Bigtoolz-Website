⏳ What is Domain Age?

Domain age is the amount of time that has passed since a domain name was first registered. It's calculated by comparing the domain's creation date (the day it was first registered) to today's date. For example, a domain registered on January 1, 2015 is roughly 11 years old as of January 2026.

Domain age is one of the many signals used by search engines, buyers, and SEO professionals to evaluate a website's credibility, history, and value. Older domains are often considered more trustworthy because they've had time to build backlinks, content, and reputation though domain age alone doesn't guarantee better rankings.

📊 Why Domain Age Matters

🔍 For SEO

Google has stated domain age is not a direct ranking factor. However, older domains tend to have:

  • More accumulated backlinks from years of content
  • Higher domain authority through age and trust signals
  • Longer content history (more indexed pages)
  • Better chances of ranking for competitive keywords

💼 For Buying a Domain

When purchasing an aged domain, check its history carefully. A 15-year-old domain might have:

  • Positive history: Clean backlinks, quality content, good reputation
  • Negative history: Spam, penalties, malware, or adult content in the past

Age alone doesn't make a domain valuable you need to check its backlink profile and content history separately.

💎 For Domain Valuation

Aged domains with clean histories typically sell for higher prices. Two-year-old domains are common; 20-year-old domains with premium TLDs can be worth thousands to millions of dollars.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Check the wayback machine (web.archive.org) to see how the domain was used historically
  • Verify backlinks with Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Moz to spot spammy link profiles
  • Check expiry date: Domains expiring soon may have owners who forgot to renew
  • Look for privacy protection: Hidden WHOIS can be legit (GDPR) or suspicious
  • Compare age with content: A 10-year-old domain with only 5 pages is unusual
  • Monitor drops: Expired aged domains can sometimes be registered at regular price
