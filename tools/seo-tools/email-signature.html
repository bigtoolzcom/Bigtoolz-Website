✉️ What is an Email Signature Generator?

An Email Signature Generator creates a professional HTML signature that you can paste into Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, or any email client. A good signature isn't just your name it's your digital business card. It appears at the bottom of every email you send and communicates who you are, what you do, and how to reach you.

Many professionals use image-only signatures that look beautiful but cause problems: they don't render when images are blocked, aren't clickable, and hurt email deliverability. Our generator creates lightweight HTML signatures that work everywhere clickable links, no image dependency (unless you add a logo), and clean rendering across all email clients.

📧 How to Add to Gmail

  1. Copy HTML from this tool
  2. Open Gmail → click the gear icon (Settings)
  3. Click See all settings → scroll to Signature
  4. Click Create new → name it (e.g., "Work")
  5. Paste the HTML (Ctrl+V / Cmd+V) into the signature box
  6. Scroll down → choose your signature as default for New emails and Replies
  7. Click Save Changes

📧 How to Add to Outlook

  1. Open Outlook → File → Options
  2. Click Mail → Signatures
  3. Click New → give it a name
  4. Paste your HTML signature
  5. Set as default for new messages and replies → OK

💡 Email Signature Best Practices

  • Keep it under 5 lines. Long signatures get ignored. Essential info only.
  • Use HTML, not images. Images don't render when blocked and hurt deliverability.
  • Test in every client. Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, and mobile render differently.
  • Link your name to your site. Increases referral traffic every email is marketing.
  • Include only 3-4 social links max. Too many looks spammy.
  • Match your brand color. Reinforces brand recognition.
  • Don't include quotes or inspirational messages. Waste of space nobody reads them.
  • Update seasonally. Refresh any promo or event lines every few months.
  • Use "&" sparingly. Some clients misrender special characters. Test yours.
