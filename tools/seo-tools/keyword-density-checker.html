# Keyword Count Density Status

📊 What is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a specific keyword or phrase appears in a piece of content compared to the total word count. It was one of the earliest ranking factors in SEO back when search engines simply counted keyword matches.

Today, Google uses semantic search and AI to understand topics, not just keywords. Density is no longer a direct ranking factor but checking it is still valuable for detecting keyword stuffing and ensuring your content reads naturally to humans.

📐 The Keyword Density Formula

Density % = (keyword occurrences ÷ total words) × 100

Example: "SEO" appears 6 times in a 300-word article
Density = (6 ÷ 300) × 100 = 2%

🔍 1-gram, 2-gram, 3-gram Explained

1-gram (Single Words)

Individual words like "SEO", "content", "optimization". Useful for spotting which individual words dominate your content but doesn't tell you the topic.

2-gram (Two-Word Phrases)

Two-word combinations like "SEO tools", "keyword density". These reveal real phrases and are much better for topic analysis.

3-gram (Three-Word Phrases)

Three-word phrases like "best SEO tools", "keyword density checker". The most accurate way to see the topic of your content. If your main 3-gram doesn't match your target keyword, your content may be off-topic.

💡 Best Practices

  • Aim for 1-2% primary keyword density. Natural writing usually lands here without effort.
  • Use synonyms and LSI keywords. Instead of repeating "SEO" 20 times, use "search optimization," "ranking," "visibility," etc.
  • Check 2-gram and 3-gram. If your main topic phrase appears rarely, your content may be unfocused.
  • Prioritize readability. If the content sounds repetitive when read aloud, it's over-optimized.
  • Ignore old rules. "1% per keyword" was never an official rule modern SEO uses topic modeling.
  • Use Google's own data. Check Search Console to see what keywords Google thinks you rank for.
