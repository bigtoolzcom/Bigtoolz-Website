🏆 What is Domain Authority (DA)?

Domain Authority (DA) is a 0-100 metric developed by Moz that predicts how well a website will rank on search engine result pages (SERPs). The higher the DA, the more "authoritative" the domain is considered and generally, the easier it is for pages on that domain to rank.

DA is calculated using a machine learning model that evaluates hundreds of signals, primarily the quantity and quality of backlinks pointing to a domain. It's one of the most popular comparative SEO metrics, used by marketers to benchmark their site against competitors and estimate link-building difficulty.

Important: DA is not a Google ranking factor. Google does not use DA in its algorithm. It's a Moz-proprietary metric used as a proxy for ranking potential.

🔍 Other Important SEO Metrics

Trust Flow (TF)

A Majestic metric (0-100) that measures how trustworthy a domain is based on the quality of sites linking to it. High TF means links come from reputable sources. Combined with Citation Flow (CF), it forms Majestic's Trust Ratio.

Domain Rating (DR)

Ahrefs's version of DA. Based purely on backlink data specifically the number and quality of unique referring domains. DR scales 0-100.

Spam Score

A Moz metric (0-17%) that estimates how likely a domain is to be penalized by Google. 0-2% = safe. 3-5% = caution. 6%+ = risky (likely spam or PBN).

Backlinks & Referring Domains

Backlinks = total number of links pointing to the domain. Referring domains = unique websites with at least one link. Referring domains is the more important metric 100 links from 100 sites is better than 1000 links from 1 site.

💡 How to Improve Your DA

  • Build high-quality backlinks guest posts, HARO, digital PR, and content that naturally attracts links.
  • Create linkable assets original research, data studies, free tools, comprehensive guides.
  • Disavow toxic links remove or disavow low-quality backlinks from spam sites and PBNs.
  • Improve technical SEO fast loading, mobile-friendly, HTTPS, clean URL structure.
  • Publish consistent content regular quality content grows authority over time.
  • Internal linking connect your pages to distribute authority across the domain.
  • Guest post on DA 50+ sites a handful of high-DA backlinks can dramatically improve your score.
