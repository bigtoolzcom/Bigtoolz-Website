▥ What is a Barcode Generator?

A Barcode Generator is a tool that converts text or numbers into a machine-readable barcode the familiar pattern of black and white lines scanned at checkout counters, warehouses, and shipping docks. Barcodes are the backbone of modern retail, logistics, and inventory systems, enabling fast, error-free data capture.

Our free barcode generator supports 12 different barcode formats from the versatile CODE128 for general use, to EAN-13 and UPC-A for retail products, to ITF-14 for shipping cartons. Everything runs 100% in your browser no data sent to any server, complete privacy.

🎯 Choosing the Right Barcode Format

For General Purpose: CODE128

If you're unsure which format to use, CODE128 is almost always the right answer. It supports all 128 ASCII characters (letters, numbers, and symbols), is compact, and is the most widely supported by modern barcode scanners.

For Retail Products: EAN-13 or UPC-A

Selling products in stores? You'll need either EAN-13 (used worldwide) or UPC-A (used in US/Canada). Both require a GS1 company prefix a unique identifier issued by GS1. Without it, your barcode won't scan correctly in retail systems.

For Shipping Cartons: ITF-14

ITF-14 is the standard for outer shipping boxes and corrugated cartons. It encodes 14 digits and prints large enough to scan reliably on rough cardboard surfaces.

For Internal Inventory: CODE39

CODE39 is simple, self-checking, and easy to print on cheap labels. Perfect for internal use like asset tags, warehouse bins, and employee badges.

For Pharma: Pharmacode

Pharmaceutical companies use Pharmacode for medicine packaging. It's a numeric-only barcode used during production line verification.
