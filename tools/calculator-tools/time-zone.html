🕐 What is a Time Zone Converter?

A Time Zone Converter helps you find out what time it is in another city when you know the time in yours. It's essential for international meetings, remote work, travel planning, calling family abroad, or coordinating with clients in different countries. The world has 24 primary time zones plus several half-hour and quarter-hour offsets and the differences change with Daylight Saving Time (DST).

Our converter uses the browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API with IANA time zones (like Asia/Kolkata, America/New_York) the same database used by operating systems and servers. This means DST transitions are handled automatically, and results are always accurate to the minute.

💡 Understanding UTC and DST

What is UTC?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the world's time standard the reference point all time zones use. Each zone is defined as UTC + offset or UTC − offset. For example, India is UTC+5:30, New York is UTC−5. UTC doesn't observe DST it stays the same all year.

What is Daylight Saving Time (DST)?

DST is a seasonal clock change where many countries shift their clocks forward by 1 hour in spring and back in autumn. This means a city can be UTC+1 in winter and UTC+2 in summer. Our tool handles this automatically the result you see is always correct for the specific date you enter.

Countries that don't use DST

  • India, China, Japan, Singapore, UAE no DST at all
  • Arizona, Hawaii (USA) exempt from DST
  • Most of Africa, Russia, Brazil DST abolished
  • Queensland (Australia), Saskatchewan (Canada) no DST
