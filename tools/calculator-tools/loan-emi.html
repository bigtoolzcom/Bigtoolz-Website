🏦 What is a Loan EMI Calculator?

A Loan EMI Calculator helps you figure out the fixed monthly payment (EMI) you'll make to repay a loan. Whether you're planning to take a home loan, car loan, personal loan, education loan, or any other type of loan this calculator shows you the exact monthly amount, total interest you'll pay over the loan tenure, and a full year-wise breakdown of how your payments are applied.

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment. Once you take a loan, you pay the same amount every month for the entire tenure. Each EMI is split between principal repayment and interest and the split changes over time (more interest early on, more principal later).

📐 The EMI Formula

All loan EMIs are calculated using the same mathematical formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1)

Where:
  P = Principal loan amount
  r = Monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  n = Total number of installments (years × 12)

Example: For a ₹10,00,000 loan at 8.5% annual interest for 20 years:

  • Principal (P) = 10,00,000
  • Monthly rate (r) = 8.5 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.007083
  • Installments (n) = 20 × 12 = 240
  • EMI = 8,678 per month
  • Total interest = 10,82,720 (more than the loan itself!)

📊 Understanding the Results

Monthly EMI

The fixed amount you'll pay every month for the entire loan tenure. This amount stays constant (for fixed-rate loans).

Total Interest

The total extra amount you'll pay beyond the principal. For long-tenure loans, this can easily exceed the principal amount. Lower tenure = less total interest.

Total Payment

Principal + Total Interest = Total amount you'll pay over the entire loan.

Amortization Schedule

A year-by-year breakdown showing how much of each EMI went toward principal vs interest, and the remaining balance after each year. Early years: mostly interest. Later years: mostly principal.

💡 Tips to Reduce Total Interest

  • Choose a shorter tenure. A 15-year loan has a higher EMI but far less total interest than a 25-year loan.
  • Make prepayments. Extra payments go directly toward the principal reducing future interest substantially.
  • Refinance at a lower rate. Even a 0.5% rate reduction can save lakhs over the loan tenure.
  • Negotiate the rate. Banks often reduce rates for existing customers with good credit.
  • Round up your EMI. Paying slightly more each month adds up significantly over 20+ years.
