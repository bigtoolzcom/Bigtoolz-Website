📈 What is a SIP Calculator?

A SIP Calculator helps you figure out how much wealth you can build through a Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) in mutual funds. Enter your monthly investment, expected return rate, and tenure the calculator instantly shows your maturity amount, total invested, and wealth gained.

SIP is a method of investing a fixed amount in mutual funds at regular intervals (usually monthly). Instead of investing a large amount all at once (lumpsum), you invest small amounts regularly. This averages out market volatility and builds investment discipline that's why SIP is the most popular way to invest in mutual funds in India.

📐 The SIP Formula

SIP maturity value is calculated using the future value of an annuity formula:

M = P × [((1 + i)n − 1) / i] × (1 + i)

Where:
  M = Maturity amount
  P = Monthly SIP amount
  i = Monthly rate of return (annual % ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
  n = Total number of SIP installments (years × 12)

Example: ₹10,000 monthly SIP at 12% annual return for 10 years:

  • Monthly SIP (P) = 10,000
  • Monthly rate (i) = 12 ÷ 12 ÷ 100 = 0.01
  • Installments (n) = 10 × 12 = 120
  • Maturity = 10,000 × [((1.01)^120 − 1) / 0.01] × 1.01 = ₹23,23,391
  • Invested = ₹12,00,000
  • Returns = ₹11,23,391 (more than the investment itself!)

📊 Understanding the Results

Maturity Amount

The final value of your SIP investment after the tenure a combination of what you invested plus the returns earned through compounding.

Invested Amount

The total of all your monthly SIP payments over the tenure. For ₹10,000/month over 10 years = ₹12,00,000.

Estimated Returns

The wealth gained the difference between maturity and invested amount. In long-tenure SIPs, returns often exceed the invested amount due to compounding.

Year-wise Breakdown

Shows how your investment grows year by year. Early years: returns are small. Later years: compounding takes over and returns grow exponentially. This is why longer tenure = dramatically higher wealth.

💡 Tips for Maximum SIP Returns

  • Start early. Time is the biggest factor in compounding. Starting at 25 vs 35 can mean 2x more wealth at retirement.
  • Use step-up SIP. Increase your SIP by 10% each year (matching salary hikes) this alone can nearly double your final corpus.
  • Don't stop SIP in bear markets. Market dips let you buy more units at lower prices this is the "averaging" benefit of SIP.
  • Choose the right fund. Equity funds for 7+ year goals, hybrid for 3-7 years, debt for short-term goals.
  • Review annually. Rebalance, increase allocation, or switch funds if underperforming for 2+ years.
  • Stay invested. The biggest wealth destroyer is panic-selling during market crashes.
