🥗 What is a Macro Calculator?

A Macro Calculator tells you exactly how many grams of protein, carbohydrates, and fats you should eat each day to reach your goal whether that's losing fat, building muscle, or maintaining your weight. It combines your body stats, activity level, and goal to produce personalized macro targets.

Unlike generic "1,500 calories for everyone" advice, macro-based nutrition (IIFYM If It Fits Your Macros) gives you flexibility. You can eat any food as long as you hit your daily protein, carb, and fat targets. This makes it sustainable for real life no restrictive dieting required.

📐 How Macros Are Calculated

We use a 4-step process:

Step 1 BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor):
Male: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5
Female: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age − 161

Step 2 TDEE:
TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor (1.2 to 1.9)

Step 3 Goal Adjustment:
Lose: TDEE − 500 Gain: TDEE + 250-500
Maintain: TDEE (unchanged)

Step 4 Macro Grams:
Protein (g) = (Cal × Protein%) / 4
Carbs (g) = (Cal × Carbs%) / 4
Fat (g) = (Cal × Fat%) / 9

Why Mifflin-St Jeor?

The Mifflin-St Jeor equation is considered the most accurate BMR formula for the general population, with an error rate of ±10% for most people. It's more reliable than the older Harris-Benedict formula and simpler than Katch-McArdle (which requires body fat %).

🍽️ The 3 Macros Explained

🍖 Protein (4 kcal/g)

Protein is the most important macro for body composition. It builds and repairs muscle, keeps you full, and has the highest thermic effect (you burn ~20-30% of protein calories just digesting it). Target: 1.6-2.2g per kg bodyweight for active adults.

🍞 Carbohydrates (4 kcal/g)

Carbs are your body's primary fuel especially for high-intensity exercise. They're not "bad" they're just easy to overeat. Prioritize complex carbs (oats, rice, vegetables) over simple sugars. Target: 2-4g per kg bodyweight.

🥑 Fats (9 kcal/g)

Fats are essential for hormone production (including testosterone), brain function, and absorbing vitamins A, D, E, K. Fats have the highest calorie density, so portions matter. Target: 0.6-1g per kg bodyweight.

📊 Common Macro Splits

  • Balanced (30/40/30): Good general-purpose split for most people.
  • High-Protein (40/30/30): Better for fat loss and muscle building.
  • Low-Carb (30/20/50): Popular for fat loss and appetite control.
  • Keto (25/5/70): Very low carb, high fat ketosis-based.

💡 Tips for Macro Success

  • Hit protein first it's the most important macro for body composition.
  • Track for 2 weeks to learn portion sizes then you can eyeball it.
  • Use a food scale eye-balling portions is usually off by 20-30%.
  • Don't stress over daily hits weekly average matters more than exact daily numbers.
  • Adjust based on progress if losing too fast, add 100-200 calories. If plateaued, reduce by 100-200.
  • Sleep and water matter even perfect macros won't work if you're sleep-deprived.
  • Consistency > perfection 80% adherence for 6 months beats 100% for 2 weeks.
