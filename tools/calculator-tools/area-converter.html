📐 What is an Area Converter?

An Area Converter converts surface area measurements between different units. It's essential for real estate, land transactions, construction, agriculture, architecture, and interior design. Whether you're buying property, planning construction, or reading land records you'll need to convert between different area units.

Our converter handles 14 units across three systems: metric (mm², cm², m², km², hectare), imperial (in², ft², yd², acre, mi²), and traditional Indian units (bigha, guntha, cent, ground) that are still used in property records today.

📊 Area Conversion Factors

Metric Units

  • 1 m² = 10,000 cm² = 1,000,000 mm²
  • 1 km² = 1,000,000 m² = 100 hectares
  • 1 hectare = 10,000 m² = 2.47105 acres

Imperial Units

  • 1 ft² = 144 in² = 0.092903 m²
  • 1 yd² = 9 ft² = 0.836127 m²
  • 1 acre = 43,560 ft² = 4,046.86 m²
  • 1 mi² = 640 acres = 2.58999 km²

Indian Land Units

  • 1 bigha ≈ 27,000 sq ft (varies by state!)
  • 1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft = 101.17 m²
  • 1 cent = 435.6 sq ft = 40.4686 m²
  • 1 ground = 2,400 sq ft = 222.967 m² (Tamil Nadu)

🌏 Understanding Indian Land Units

Unlike the metric and imperial systems, Indian land units vary significantly by region. This causes confusion in property transactions. Always confirm which definition your local authority uses.

Bigha Varies by State

  • Uttar Pradesh (Pucca): 27,000 sq ft (2,508 m²)
  • Bihar (Kaccha): 27,220 sq ft (2,529 m²)
  • Bihar (Pucca): 32,400 sq ft (3,010 m²)
  • West Bengal: 14,400 sq ft (1,338 m²)
  • Rajasthan: 27,225 sq ft (2,529 m²)
  • Madhya Pradesh: 12,000 sq ft (1,115 m²)

Guntha Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat

1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft (101.17 m²). Standard across these states. 40 gunthas = 1 acre.

Cent South India

1 cent = 435.6 sq ft (40.47 m²). 100 cents = 1 acre. Common in Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Karnataka.

Ground Tamil Nadu

1 ground = 2,400 sq ft (222.967 m²). Common in Chennai real estate. 24 grounds = 1 acre (approximately).
