⚖️ What is a Weight Converter?

A Weight Converter (also called a Mass Converter) is a tool that converts weight or mass measurements between different units. It's essential for cooking, shipping, fitness tracking, international trade, jewelry, and any situation where metric and imperial units meet.

Our converter supports 12 weight units from the tiny microgram (used in pharmacology) to the massive metric ton (used in shipping). It handles all standard metric units (μg, mg, g, kg, t), imperial units (oz, lb, stone, tons), plus carats (for gemstones) and grains (for ammunition and pharmacology).

📐 Common Weight Conversion Factors

  • 1 kilogram = 1,000 g = 2.20462 lb = 35.274 oz

  • 1 ounce = 28.3495 g (regular) / 31.1035 g (troy)
  • 1 stone = 14 lb = 6.35029 kg
  • 1 metric ton = 1,000 kg = 2,204.62 lb
  • 1 US ton = 2,000 lb = 907.185 kg
  • 1 UK long ton = 2,240 lb = 1,016.05 kg
  • 1 carat = 0.2 g = 200 mg (exact)

  • 1 microgram = 1/1,000,000 g

💡 Interesting Weight Facts

  • The kilogram was redefined in 2019 no longer based on a physical metal cylinder in France. Now defined by the Planck constant.
  • The "ounce" comes from Latin "uncia" = 1/12. A Roman ounce was 1/12 of a Roman pound.
  • The "pound" comes from Latin "libra pondo" that's why its abbreviation is "lb" (from libra), not "p".
  • Carat vs Karat: "Carat" (ct) is weight for gemstones. "Karat" (K) is gold purity (24K = 99.9% gold).
  • The stone is still used in UK and Ireland for body weight. A 10-stone person weighs 63.5 kg.
  • A grain originally meant the weight of a single grain of cereal. Still used in ammunition (a 9mm bullet weighs ~115 grains).
  • Earth weighs about 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg that's roughly 6 septillion metric tons.
  • A cubic meter of water weighs exactly 1,000 kg (1 metric ton) at 4°C this was the original definition of the kilogram!

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1 kilogram = 2.20462 pounds exactly. So 70 kg = 154.324 lb. For pounds to kg: 1 lb = 0.453592 kg. So 150 lb = 68.039 kg. Use our converter for exact figures.

1 ounce (avoirdupois) = 28.3495 grams exactly. But a troy ounce (for gold/silver) = 31.1035 grams. Regular ounces are lighter. That's why a 1 oz gold coin weighs more than 1 oz of flour.

1 stone = 14 pounds = 6.35029 kilograms. Used in UK and Ireland for body weight. Example: a person weighing 10 stone weighs 63.5 kg or 140 lb. A 12-stone person = 76.2 kg.

Three different "tons" exist: Metric ton (tonne) = 1,000 kg = 2,204.62 lb. US ton (short ton) = 2,000 lb = 907.185 kg. UK ton (long ton) = 2,240 lb = 1,016.05 kg. Metric ton is heaviest, US is lightest. Always check which "ton" is used!

1 carat = 0.2 grams (200 mg) exactly. So 1 gram = 5 carats. Used for gemstones and pearls. A 1-carat diamond weighs 200 mg. Note: "karat" (with 'k') is different it measures gold purity (24K = pure gold), not weight.

Quick approximation: multiply kg by 2.2. Example: 70 kg × 2.2 = 154 lb (actual: 154.324). For lb to kg: divide by 2.2 or multiply by 0.45. Example: 150 lb × 0.45 = 67.5 kg (actual: 68.039).

Yes our converter uses NIST-standard conversion factors with high precision. The kg-to-lb factor (2.20462), ounce-to-gram (28.3495), and metric ton (1000 kg) are exact by international agreement.
