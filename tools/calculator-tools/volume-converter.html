🧪 What is a Volume Converter?

A Volume Converter converts capacity or volume measurements between different units. It's essential for cooking, baking, chemistry, medicine, fuel, shipping, and any task where liquid or dry capacity matters. Whether you're following a US recipe in a metric kitchen or calculating fuel capacity across borders a volume converter saves time and prevents mistakes.

Our converter handles 15 units across four systems: metric (ml, L, cm³, m³), imperial/US (gal, qt, pt, cup, fl oz), UK imperial (UK gallon), cubic imperial (ft³, in³), plus the oil barrel used in the petroleum industry.

📊 Volume Conversion Factors

Metric Units

  • 1 m³ = 1,000 L = 1,000,000 ml = 1,000,000 cm³
  • 1 L = 1,000 ml = 1,000 cm³ = 0.001 m³
  • 1 cm³ = 1 ml (exactly the same)

US Liquid Volume

  • 1 US gallon = 3.78541 L = 128 US fl oz
  • 1 US quart = 1/4 gal = 0.946353 L
  • 1 US pint = 1/2 quart = 0.473176 L
  • 1 US cup = 8 fl oz = 236.588 ml
  • 1 US fl oz = 29.5735 ml
  • 1 US tbsp = 14.7868 ml = 1/2 fl oz
  • 1 US tsp = 4.92892 ml = 1/3 tbsp

UK Imperial

  • 1 UK gallon = 4.54609 L (about 20% larger than US)
  • 1 UK fl oz = 28.4131 ml

Cubic Imperial

  • 1 ft³ = 28.3168 L = 7.48052 US gal
  • 1 in³ = 16.3871 ml

Industrial

  • 1 oil barrel = 158.987 L = 42 US gallons

🍳 Cooking Conversions Cheat Sheet

Cooking is where volume conversions matter most especially when recipes jump between metric and US measurements. Here are the essentials:

  • 1 cup (US) = 236.588 ml ≈ 240 ml
  • 1 cup (metric) = 250 ml (used in Australia, NZ, Canada)
  • 1 cup (UK) = 284.13 ml (rarely used today)
  • 1 tbsp (US) = 14.7868 ml ≈ 15 ml
  • 1 tbsp (metric) = 15 ml
  • 1 tbsp (UK) = 17.7582 ml
  • 1 tsp (US) = 4.92892 ml ≈ 5 ml
  • 1 fl oz = 29.5735 ml (US)
  • 1 pint (US) = 473.176 ml
  • 1 quart (US) = 946.353 ml

💡 Interesting Volume Facts

  • 1 litre of water weighs exactly 1 kg at 4°C this was the original definition of the kilogram.
  • A US gallon is smaller than a UK gallon 3.785 L vs 4.546 L, a 20% difference.
  • A barrel of oil is 42 US gallons a standard set in Pennsylvania in 1866.
  • The human body contains about 5 litres of blood.
  • A bathtub holds about 150-300 litres of water.
  • An Olympic swimming pool holds 2.5 million litres nearly 660,000 gallons.
  • A teardrop is about 0.05 ml 20 drops per ml.
  • A raindrop varies from 0.05 ml (drizzle) to 0.5 ml (heavy rain).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1 US gallon = 3.78541 litres exactly. So 10 gallons = 37.854 L. For UK/Imperial: 1 imperial gallon = 4.54609 litres. US gallons are about 17% smaller than UK gallons.

1 US cup = 236.588 ml (8 US fl oz). 1 metric cup (Australia, NZ, Canada) = 250 ml. 1 UK cup = 284.13 ml. US cups are smaller than metric and UK cups this matters a lot in baking!

1 litre = 33.814 US fluid ounces = 35.195 UK fluid ounces. Note: fluid ounces (volume) are different from ounces (weight) 1 fl oz of water weighs ~1 oz, but the same fl oz of oil weighs less. Our converter uses fluid ounces for volume.

1 litre = 1,000 cubic centimeters (cm³) = 1,000 millilitres. A cm³ is exactly the same as a millilitre (ml). A 1-litre bottle holds 1,000 cm³ of liquid.

Fluid ounce (fl oz) measures VOLUME. Ounce (oz) measures WEIGHT/MASS. 1 US fluid ounce = 29.5735 ml of liquid. 1 ounce of weight = 28.3495 grams. They're only equal for water at 4°C for other liquids they differ based on density.

1 US cup = 16 US tablespoons = 48 US teaspoons = 8 US fluid ounces. For metric: 1 metric cup (250 ml) = ~16.67 metric tablespoons (15 ml each). Standard US: 1 tbsp = 3 tsp.

Yes our converter uses NIST-exact conversion factors. US liquid volumes (gallon, quart, cup, fl oz, tbsp, tsp) and metric volumes are standardised. Results are accurate to 6+ decimal places.
