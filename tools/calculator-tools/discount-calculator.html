🏷 What is a Discount Calculator?

A Discount Calculator helps you figure out the final price after a discount, the amount you save, and the effective discount percentage. Whether you're shopping online, negotiating a deal, planning a sale, or preparing an invoice this tool eliminates mental math and gives you accurate answers instantly.

Our calculator supports three calculation modes: find the final price (from original + discount %), find the discount percentage (from original + final price), and find the original price (from final + discount %). It also handles stacked discounts the tricky case where multiple discounts apply in sequence.

📐 The Discount Formulas

Three key formulas drive all discount calculations:

1. Find Final Price

Discount Amount = Original × Rate / 100
Final Price = Original − Discount Amount

Example: ₹1,000 at 20% off = 1000 × 20/100 = ₹200 off
Final = 1000 − 200 = ₹800

2. Find Discount Percentage

Discount % = (Original − Final) / Original × 100

Example: Original ₹1,000, Final ₹750
Discount = (1000 − 750) / 1000 × 100 = 25% off

3. Find Original Price

Original = Final × 100 / (100 − Discount %)

Example: Final ₹800 at 20% off → Original = 800 × 100 / 80 = ₹1,000

⚠️ Stacked Discounts The Big Mistake

This is the most common discount mistake shoppers and sellers make:

Wrong: "10% + 20% = 30% off"

Right: Apply 10% first, then 20% on the reduced amount.

  • Original: ₹1,000
  • After 10% off: ₹1,000 × 0.90 = ₹900
  • After 20% off on ₹900: ₹900 × 0.80 = ₹720
  • Effective discount: (1000 − 720) / 1000 = 28%, not 30%

Our calculator handles stacked discounts correctly add each discount separately and it applies them in sequence.

💡 Discount Tips

  • Tax comes after discount. Always apply discount first, then calculate tax on the reduced price.
  • Watch the fine print. "Up to 50% off" often means only a few items are at 50%; others are at less.
  • MRP vs Sale Price. In India, discounts are calculated on MRP (Maximum Retail Price), not on the regular selling price.
  • Round to psychological prices. ₹999 looks much cheaper than ₹1,000 a classic retail tactic.
  • Compare effective price. 20% off + free shipping beats 25% off + ₹100 shipping if shipping exceeds the savings.
  • Check return policies. A discount is only worth it if the product works for you.
