🎂 What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator finds the exact difference between your date of birth and any target date (usually today). Unlike a rough "how old are you" estimate, our calculator uses precise calendar math accounting for leap years, varying month lengths, and today's date to give you your exact age down to days, hours, minutes, and seconds.

You can also use this tool to calculate age at any specific date past or future. Useful for filling forms, checking age eligibility, planning birthdays, or just out of curiosity.

📐 What You Get

Exact Age

The main result shows your age as years + months + days like "25 years, 3 months, 12 days". This is the format used in most official forms and applications.

Age in Different Units

  • Months: Total months you've been alive.
  • Weeks: Total weeks lived (some people celebrate weekly milestones!).
  • Days: Total days alive a popular stat for newborns and kids.
  • Hours, minutes, seconds: For fun, precise milestones.

Day of Birth

The day of the week (Monday, Tuesday, etc.) you were born on. Popular for trivia and horoscopes.

Zodiac Sign

Your Western sun sign based on birth date Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, or Pisces.

Next Birthday Countdown

Days until your next birthday plus the exact date (e.g., "Monday, 15 March 2026").
