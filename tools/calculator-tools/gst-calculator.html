₹ What is a GST Calculator?

A GST Calculator is a simple tool that computes the Goods and Services Tax on any amount. GST is India's unified indirect tax introduced on July 1, 2017, replacing a complex web of taxes like VAT, Service Tax, CST, and Excise Duty. Today, every business that sells goods or services in India needs to calculate GST for invoices, quotes, and filings.

Our free GST calculator handles both scenarios: adding GST to a base amount (exclusive pricing) and removing GST from an inclusive price to find the base. It also splits the tax into CGST + SGST (for intra-state sales) or shows IGST (for inter-state sales) essential for invoicing correctly.

📐 The GST Formula

There are two formulas depending on whether the amount includes GST or not:

Add GST (Exclusive → Inclusive)

GST Amount = Base × Rate / 100
Total = Base + GST Amount

Example: ₹10,000 × 18/100 = ₹1,800 GST
Total = ₹10,000 + ₹1,800 = ₹11,800

Remove GST (Inclusive → Exclusive)

Base = Total × 100 / (100 + Rate)
GST Amount = Total − Base

Example: ₹11,800 × 100 / 118 = ₹10,000 base
GST = ₹11,800 − ₹10,000 = ₹1,800

🎯 CGST, SGST and IGST Explained

India's GST is a destination-based tax, split differently based on whether the sale is within a state or across states:

Intra-State Sale (Within Same State)

The GST is split equally between the Central Government and the State Government:

  • CGST = Central GST (goes to central government)
  • SGST = State GST (goes to state government)
  • Example: 18% GST → 9% CGST + 9% SGST

Inter-State Sale (Between Different States)

The full GST is charged as IGST (Integrated GST) and later divided between the consuming state and the center by the central government:

  • IGST = Integrated GST (full rate)
  • Example: 18% GST → 18% IGST

💡 Quick Tips

  • Always check supply type. Wrong CGST/SGST vs IGST split causes invoice rejection by buyers.
  • HSN/SAC codes matter. Every product has an HSN (goods) or SAC (services) code that determines its GST rate.
  • Composition scheme: Small businesses can opt for a flat rate (1-6%) instead of normal GST no input credit.
  • Reverse charge: For some services (like from unregistered dealers), the buyer pays GST, not the seller.
  • GST on discounts: Trade discounts BEFORE sale reduce GST; discounts AFTER sale don't (unless known in advance).
  • B2B vs B2C: B2B invoices need GSTIN of buyer; B2C don't.
