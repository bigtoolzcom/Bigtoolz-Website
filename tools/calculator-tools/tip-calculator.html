💵 What is a Tip Calculator?

A Tip Calculator helps you quickly compute the tip amount on any bill and split it among your group. Whether you're at a restaurant, paying for a taxi, ordering food delivery, or at a bar this calculator eliminates mental math and ensures you leave the right amount every time.

Our calculator does three things at once: calculates the tip at any percentage, adds it to your bill to show the total, and splits it among any number of people so everyone knows their exact share. No signup, no math errors, 100% free.

📐 The Tip Formula

Tip calculation is simple but splitting adds one extra step:

Tip = Bill × Rate / 100
Total = Bill + Tip
Per Person = Total / Number of People

Example: ₹1,000 bill at 18% tip, split among 4
Tip = 1000 × 18/100 = ₹180
Total = 1000 + 180 = ₹1,180
Per person = 1180 / 4 = ₹295

💡 Tipping Tips

  • 15-20% for restaurant service. Below 15% signals dissatisfaction in the US. 20%+ for excellent service.
  • Tip on the pre-tax amount (US etiquette) but post-tax is fine too. Difference is usually small.
  • Cash tips are appreciated more. In some countries (US), credit card tips can be delayed or taxed differently.
  • Delivery apps: Tip the driver, not the app. Many apps pass tips fully, but some don't check.
  • Buffet vs full service: Buffet 10%, full service 15-20%. Self-service: no tip.
  • Check for service charge: Many European and Indian restaurants add 5-10% service charge to the bill. If it's there, no extra tip needed.
  • Split evenly vs proportional: Splitting evenly is common; splitting by what each person ordered is fairer but more complex.
