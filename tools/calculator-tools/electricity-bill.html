⚡ What is an Electricity Bill Calculator?

An Electricity Bill Calculator estimates your monthly power bill based on your consumption (in kWh or "units") and your tariff structure. It's essential for budget planning, comparing appliances, evaluating solar ROI, and understanding why your bill varies month to month.

Our calculator handles all common tariff structures: flat rate, slab-based (tiered), and custom rates. It also lets you add fixed charges (meter rent, service charges) and taxes/duties that appear on real bills giving you an accurate estimate of what you'll actually pay.

📐 How Electricity Bills Are Calculated

The core formula varies based on tariff structure:

Flat Rate Tariff

Bill = (Units × Rate) + Fixed Charges + Tax

Example: 250 units × ₹8 + ₹100 + 5% tax = ₹2,205

Slab / Tiered Tariff

Energy Charge = Σ (Units in each slab × Slab rate)
Total Bill = Energy Charge + Fixed Charges + Tax

Example (India-style slabs):
  0-100 units @ ₹3 = ₹300
  101-200 units @ ₹5 = ₹500
  201-500 units @ ₹7 = ₹350 (50 units)
  Energy Charge = ₹1,150
  + Fixed ₹100 + 5% tax = ₹1,313 total

💡 Reducing Your Electricity Bill

  • Switch to LED bulbs. LEDs use 75-80% less power than incandescent. Replacing 10 bulbs saves ~₹500/month.
  • Set AC to 24-26°C. Each degree lower adds ~6% to AC power consumption. 24°C is optimal.
  • Service your AC yearly. Dirty filters increase power use by 5-15%.
  • Use 5-star BEE appliances. A 5-star AC uses 20-30% less than 3-star over its lifetime.
  • Unplug idle devices. Standby power can be 5-10% of your bill.
  • Use ceiling fans with AC. Fans circulate cool air, letting you set AC higher.
  • Run heavy appliances off-peak. Washing machine, dishwasher, water heater run at night if TOU tariff.
  • Consider solar panels. A 3kW rooftop system can offset most household usage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Bill = (Units × Rate per unit) + Fixed Charges + Taxes. For slab-based tariffs (common in India), each slab's units are charged at that slab's rate. Our calculator handles both flat and slab-based tariffs automatically.

1 unit = 1 kWh (kilowatt-hour) = 1,000 watts used for 1 hour. Example: a 100W bulb running for 10 hours = 1 unit. A 1,500W AC running for 1 hour = 1.5 units.

Slab rates are tiered pricing where the rate per unit increases with higher consumption. Common in India: 0-100 units at ₹3/unit, 101-200 at ₹5/unit, 201-500 at ₹7/unit, 500+ at ₹9/unit. Higher usage = higher rate per unit this encourages conservation.

Use energy-efficient appliances (5-star BEE rated), switch to LED lights, set AC to 24-26°C, unplug idle devices, run heavy appliances during off-peak hours, service AC/fridge regularly, and consider rooftop solar. Simple changes can cut bills by 20-30%.

kW (kilowatt) is power the rate of energy use at any instant. kWh (kilowatt-hour) is energy the total amount used over time. kW × hours = kWh. You're billed for kWh (units), not kW.

Fixed charges are a flat monthly fee regardless of consumption covering meter rent, connection charges, and grid maintenance. In India it ranges from ₹30-200/month for residential. In the US, "customer charge" is usually $5-15/month.

Our calculator uses the exact formula your utility uses for flat and slab tariffs. For accuracy, enter your state's current slab rates (available on your electricity board's website). Real bills may include additional items like electricity duty, meter rent, FPPCA, or subsidy adjustments not covered here.
