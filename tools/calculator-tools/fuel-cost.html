⛽ What is a Fuel Cost Calculator?

A Fuel Cost Calculator helps you estimate the fuel needed for a trip and its cost. Whether you're planning a road trip, calculating the running cost of your car, or estimating monthly fuel expenses, this tool gives you accurate numbers in seconds.

Our calculator supports both metric (km, litres, kmpl) and imperial (miles, gallons, mpg) units plus the popular L/100km format used in Europe. You can calculate one-way or round-trip costs, split fuel among passengers, and see the exact cost per km/mile.

📐 The Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel cost calculation uses a simple three-step formula:

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Fuel Efficiency
Total Fuel Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price
Cost per km = Fuel Price ÷ Fuel Efficiency

Example: 500 km ÷ 15 kmpl × ₹100/litre
Fuel Required = 500 ÷ 15 = 33.33 litres
Total Cost = 33.33 × 100 = ₹3,333.33
Cost per km = 100 ÷ 15 = ₹6.67/km

💡 Tips to Save Fuel

  • Maintain steady speeds. 60-80 km/h is the sweet spot for most cars. Rapid acceleration and braking waste fuel.
  • Check tyre pressure weekly. Under-inflated tyres increase fuel consumption by 3-5%.
  • Reduce AC usage. AC can drop efficiency by 10-20%, especially in city traffic.
  • Remove extra weight. Every 50 kg of unnecessary cargo increases fuel use by ~2%.
  • Use cruise control on highways. Steady speed burns less fuel than constant throttle adjustments.
  • Plan routes to avoid traffic. Stop-and-go driving is the biggest fuel waster.
  • Service regularly. Dirty air filters, old spark plugs, and low oil increase fuel consumption.
  • Combine trips. A warm engine is more efficient than multiple cold starts.
