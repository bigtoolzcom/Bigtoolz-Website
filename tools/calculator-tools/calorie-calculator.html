🍎 What is a Calorie Calculator?

A Calorie Calculator estimates how many calories your body needs per day to maintain, lose, or gain weight. It's based on your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate the calories you burn at complete rest) and your TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure BMR plus all your daily activities). The result is your maintenance calorie target, which you can adjust up or down based on your goals.

Whether you're trying to lose weight, gain muscle, or just understand your body's energy needs, this calculator gives you a personalized daily calorie target based on proven scientific formulas.

📐 Understanding the Numbers

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate)

The calories your body needs just to stay alive breathing, circulating blood, maintaining temperature. This is what you'd burn if you stayed in bed all day.

TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure)

Your actual daily calorie burn. TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor. For a moderately active person, TDEE is usually 1.4-1.6× BMR.

BMI (Body Mass Index)

A rough measure of body composition based on height and weight. Categories: Under 18.5 = Underweight, 18.5-24.9 = Normal, 25-29.9 = Overweight, 30+ = Obese.

🎯 Calorie Goals Explained

  • Lose weight: TDEE − 500 cal/day = ~0.5 kg (1 lb) loss per week.
  • Maintain: Eat at TDEE = weight stays stable.
  • Gain weight: TDEE + 500 cal/day = ~0.5 kg gain per week.
  • Aggressive loss: TDEE − 1,000 cal/day = ~1 kg loss per week (not recommended long-term).

🥗 Macros Explained

Macros (macronutrients) are protein, carbs, and fat. The right ratio depends on your goal:

  • Weight loss: Higher protein (35%), moderate carbs (35%), lower fat (30%) preserves muscle while losing fat.
  • Maintenance: Balanced (30/40/30) standard healthy ratio.
  • Muscle gain: Higher protein (30%), higher carbs (45%), moderate fat (25%) fuels workouts and recovery.

⚠️ Important Notes

  • Calculations are estimates with ±10% accuracy. Real needs vary by individual metabolism.
  • Track your actual intake and weight for 2-3 weeks. If weight isn't changing as expected, adjust by 100-200 calories.
  • Not suitable for pregnant/breastfeeding women, children under 18, or people with medical conditions.
  • Consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personalized medical nutrition advice.
