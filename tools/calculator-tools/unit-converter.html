📏 What is a Unit Converter?

A Unit Converter is a tool that converts a measurement from one unit to another. It's essential when working with recipes, scientific calculations, international measurements, engineering, construction, or any task where different unit systems meet.

Our converter supports 10 major categories Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data, Pressure, and Energy with dozens of units in each. All conversions happen instantly in your browser, no upload, no signup.

🌍 Why Do We Need Unit Converters?

Two major measurement systems are in use worldwide: the Metric system (metres, kilograms, litres used in most countries) and the Imperial system (feet, pounds, gallons used in USA, UK, and a few others). Whenever you work across these systems, conversions are necessary.

Common scenarios: A US recipe says "2 cups" but you have a metric measuring cup. A European hotel lists "180 cm bed" but you think in feet. A scientific paper gives pressure in "psi" but your equipment reads "kPa". Unit converters save time and prevent expensive mistakes.

🌡 Temperature Conversions

Temperature is special it's not a simple multiplication. Use these formulas:

  • Celsius to Fahrenheit: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • Fahrenheit to Celsius: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • Celsius to Kelvin: K = °C + 273.15
  • Kelvin to Celsius: °C = K − 273.15
  • Fahrenheit to Kelvin: K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

💡 Interesting Unit Facts

  • The metre was originally defined as 1/10,000,000 of the distance from the North Pole to the equator.
  • The kilogram was redefined in 2019 using the Planck constant no longer a physical prototype.
  • The mile comes from the Roman "mille passus" (1,000 paces). A Roman pace was 5 feet.
  • US and UK gallons are different: US gallon = 3.785 L, UK imperial gallon = 4.546 L. That's a 20% difference!
  • A troy ounce (used for gold) = 31.103 grams. A regular ounce = 28.349 grams.
  • Nautical miles are based on 1 minute of latitude ≈ 1.852 km. That's why ships and planes use them.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

A unit converter is a tool that converts a value from one measurement unit to another. Examples: 1 km = 0.621371 miles, 1 kg = 2.20462 pounds, 100°C = 212°F. Essential for international work, science, cooking, and travel.

Formula: °F = (°C × 9/5) + 32. Example: 25°C → (25 × 9/5) + 32 = 77°F. For F to C: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. Our temperature converter handles all combinations automatically.

1 ounce (oz) = 28.3495 grams. 1 pound (lb) = 453.592 grams = 16 ounces. 1 kilogram (kg) = 1000 grams = 2.20462 pounds. For gold/silver, a "troy ounce" = 31.103 grams (different from regular ounce).

US gallon = 3.78541 litres. Imperial (UK) gallon = 4.54609 litres. The UK gallon is about 20% larger. Always check which gallon is used especially for fuel economy figures (US mpg vs UK mpg).

Two definitions: Decimal (SI): 1 GB = 1,000,000,000 bytes (1000 MB). Binary (IEC): 1 GiB = 1,073,741,824 bytes (1024 MiB). Storage manufacturers use decimal (GB); Windows often shows binary (GiB) but labels it "GB" that's why a "500 GB" drive shows as ~465 GB in Windows.
