📐 The 4 Modes Explained

1. X% of Y Percentage of Value

Finds what a percentage of a number is. Formula: (X × Y) ÷ 100. Example: 15% of 200 = (15 × 200) ÷ 100 = 30. Use it for discounts, tips, commissions.

2. X is what % of Y Find Percentage

Finds what percentage one number is of another. Formula: (X ÷ Y) × 100. Example: 45 is what % of 180? (45 ÷ 180) × 100 = 25%. Use it for test scores, market share, progress tracking.

3. Percentage Increase / Decrease

Finds the percentage change from old to new value. Formula: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100. Example: 100 → 150 = ((150 − 100) ÷ 100) × 100 = +50%. A negative result means a decrease.

4. Percentage Difference

Finds the difference between two values without direction. Formula: (|A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2)) × 100. Example: A=100, B=120 → (20 ÷ 110) × 100 = 18.18%. Use it to compare similar values.

🎯 Common Percentage Formulas

  • X% of Y: (X ÷ 100) × Y
  • X is what % of Y: (X ÷ Y) × 100
  • % increase: ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100
  • % decrease: ((Old − New) ÷ Old) × 100
  • % difference: (|A − B| ÷ ((A + B) ÷ 2)) × 100
  • Add X% to Y: Y × (1 + X ÷ 100)
  • Subtract X% from Y: Y × (1 − X ÷ 100)
