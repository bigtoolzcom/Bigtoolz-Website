📏 What is a Length Converter?

A Length Converter is a tool that converts distance or length measurements between different units. Whether you're reading a US recipe in inches, a European blueprint in millimetres, or planning a trip measured in miles vs kilometres a length converter gives you instant, accurate answers.

Our converter supports all common metric units (mm, cm, m, km, μm) and imperial units (inch, foot, yard, mile) plus the nautical mile used in aviation and maritime navigation. All conversions happen 100% in your browser no upload, no signup.

Common Conversion Factors

  • 1 inch = 2.54 cm = 25.4 mm (exact)
  • 1 foot = 30.48 cm = 0.3048 m (exact)
  • 1 yard = 3 feet = 0.9144 m (exact)
  • 1 mile = 1,609.344 m = 1.609344 km (exact)
  • 1 nautical mile = 1,852 m (exact)
  • 1 km = 1000 m = 0.621371 miles
  • 1 m = 100 cm = 1000 mm = 3.28084 feet
  • 1 cm = 10 mm = 0.393701 inches

💡 Fun Length Facts

  • The metre was originally defined in 1793 as 1/10,000,000 of the distance from the North Pole to the equator through Paris.
  • The mile comes from Latin "mille passus" = 1,000 Roman paces (a pace was 5 Roman feet).
  • A marathon is 42.195 km or 26.219 miles the distance from Marathon to Athens in ancient Greece.
  • Mount Everest is 8,848.86 m tall about 5.5 miles high.
  • A light-year is 9.46 trillion km the distance light travels in one year.
  • The "inch" was historically the width of a man's thumb (roughly 2.54 cm).
  • The "foot" was the length of an average adult foot now standardised at 30.48 cm.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

1 inch = 2.54 cm exactly. This is an internationally defined value since 1959. So 10 inches = 25.4 cm, 30 cm = 11.811 inches. Our converter uses this exact factor.

1 meter = 3.28084 feet (approximately 3 feet 3.37 inches). Conversely, 1 foot = 0.3048 meters exactly. So a 6-foot person is 1.829 m tall.

1 mile = 1.609344 km exactly. So a 5K run = 3.10686 miles. A marathon (42.195 km) = 26.219 miles. A 100-mile drive = 160.934 km.

A nautical mile = 1,852 meters exactly (1.15078 regular miles). It's based on 1 minute of latitude arc and is used in aviation and maritime navigation. Speed is measured in "knots" (nautical miles per hour).

The metric system (metres, centimetres) is used by most countries and is the international standard for science and trade. The imperial system (feet, inches, miles) is still used in the US, UK (partially), Liberia, and Myanmar. Canada and the UK use both metric for official, imperial for everyday.

Quick approximation: multiply km by 0.6 to get miles (or 5/8). Example: 100 km ≈ 60 miles. For precise: 100 km × 0.621371 = 62.1371 miles. For miles to km: multiply by 1.6 (or 8/5).

Yes our converter uses NIST-standard conversion factors with high precision. The inch-to-cm factor (2.54), foot-to-m (0.3048), and mile-to-km (1.609344) are exact by international agreement since 1959.
