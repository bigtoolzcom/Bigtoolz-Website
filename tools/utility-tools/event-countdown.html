📅 What is an Event Countdown?

An event countdown is a live timer that shows exactly how much time is left until a specific event whether it's a wedding, exam, vacation, product launch, project deadline, or holiday. It breaks down the remaining time into days, hours, minutes and seconds and updates every second, so you always know precisely how long you have.

Our free event countdown lets you save unlimited events, each with its own emoji, category, date and time. Events are auto-sorted with the nearest one at top. You get visual milestone alerts (7 days, tomorrow, today) and a progress bar showing how close you are. All data is stored locally in your browser nothing is uploaded.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Set the exact time if your flight is at 6:30 AM, don't leave it at 00:00. Precise time = accurate countdown.
  • Use categories filter events by Personal, Work, Travel to stay organized.
  • Watch the progress bar it shows what percentage of time has passed between "save date" and the event.
  • Copy event info paste into WhatsApp or calendar notes for reference.
  • Clear past events after the event, remove it to keep the list clean.
  • Bookmark this page it works offline and remembers your events.

🔒 Privacy & Storage

All events are stored in your browser's localStorage a private space on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and no one else can see your events. The countdown works fully offline after the first page load.
