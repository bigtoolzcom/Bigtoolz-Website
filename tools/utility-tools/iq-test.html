🧠 What is an IQ Test?

An IQ (Intelligence Quotient) test measures cognitive abilities like reasoning, problem-solving, pattern recognition, memory, and verbal skills. It gives you a single number that compares your performance to a large sample of the population, where 100 is the average.

Professional IQ tests like the WAIS-IV (Wechsler Adult Intelligence Scale) or Stanford-Binet are administered by licensed psychologists over 1-2 hours. Our test is a short educational screening a quick 20-question assessment for fun and self-awareness, not a clinical diagnosis.

📚 The 5 Cognitive Categories

🎯 Logic & Reasoning

Tests your ability to draw conclusions from given information syllogisms, deductions, sequences. This is the "pure thinking" part of IQ.

🔢 Mathematical Ability

Number sequences, basic arithmetic, numerical relationships. Tests fluid math reasoning, not memorized formulas.

🔷 Pattern Recognition

Sequences of numbers, letters, or shapes. Tests your brain's ability to spot rules and predict "what comes next".

📝 Verbal Reasoning

Analogies, vocabulary, word relationships. Tests language-based intelligence how well you manipulate concepts with words.

🧭 Spatial Thinking

Mental rotation, visualization, mirror images. Tests how well you can "see" things in your head.

💡 Can You Improve Your IQ?

IQ is generally stable in adults but that doesn't mean you can't improve cognitive performance:

  • Learn new skills languages, instruments, coding boost neuroplasticity.
  • Read widely fiction and non-fiction both build vocabulary and reasoning.
  • Solve puzzles chess, Sudoku, crosswords, logic puzzles.
  • Exercise regularly cardio increases blood flow to the brain and BDNF levels.
  • Sleep 7-9 hours memory consolidation happens during deep sleep.
  • Eat brain foods omega-3s, leafy greens, berries, nuts.
  • Meditate improves focus and working memory.
  • Stay social conversations are mental workouts.
