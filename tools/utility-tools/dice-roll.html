🎲 What is a Dice Roller?

A virtual dice roller is a digital replacement for physical dice and the modern way to play tabletop games, decide things randomly, or teach probability. Whether you're playing Dungeons & Dragons, board games like Yahtzee or Monopoly, teaching statistics, or just need a random 1-6 result, our dice roller gives you fair, crypto-secure, animated rolls instantly.

Unlike most online dice rollers, ours runs 100% in your browser and uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same source used by banks and security software. No bias, no predictable patterns, works offline.

🎯 Dice Types Explained

D4 Tetrahedron (1-4)

Four-sided die. Rare in games, common in some RPG damage rolls.

D6 Standard Cube (1-6)

The classic die. Used in board games, Yahtzee, Monopoly, craps, most casual games.

D8 Octahedron (1-8)

Eight-sided die. Popular in RPGs for weapon damage (especially longswords and battleaxes).

D10 Pentagonal Trapezohedron (1-10)

Ten-sided die. Also used as a percentile die (00-90) alongside a D10 for D100 rolls.

D12 Dodecahedron (1-12)

Twelve-sided die. Used for Barbarian hit points and some weapon damage in D&D.

D20 Icosahedron (1-20)

The most iconic RPG die. Used in Dungeons & Dragons for attack rolls, ability checks, and saving throws. Rolling a 20 = critical hit. Rolling a 1 = critical miss.

D100 Percentile (1-100)

Hundred-sided die or two D10s. Used for percentile rolls, random tables, and probability checks.
