🥗 What is a Diet Plan Generator?

A diet plan generator creates a personalized meal schedule based on your body stats (age, gender, height, weight), activity level, and goal. It first calculates your BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) and TDEE (Total Daily Energy Expenditure), then sets your daily calorie and macro targets, and finally spreads those calories across the day with specific meals.

Our free diet plan generator uses the Mifflin-St Jeor equation the most accurate BMR formula for the general population, according to the American Dietetic Association. All calculations run in your browser no signup, no tracking, no BS.

📐 The Formulas Behind Your Plan

1. BMR Mifflin-St Jeor

Men: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) + 5
Women: BMR = (10 × weight kg) + (6.25 × height cm) − (5 × age) − 161

BMR is the calories your body burns at complete rest. Most people burn 60-70% of their daily calories just keeping the body alive.

2. TDEE Activity Multiplier

TDEE = BMR × Activity Factor

1.2 Sedentary (desk job, little exercise)
1.375 Lightly active (1-3 days/week)
1.55 Moderately active (3-5 days/week)
1.725 Very active (6-7 days/week)
1.9 Extra active (athlete, 2×/day)

3. Calorie Target by Goal

Lose Weight: TDEE − 500 kcal (~0.5 kg/week loss)
Maintain: TDEE
Build Muscle: TDEE + 300 kcal (~0.25 kg/week gain)
Gain Weight: TDEE + 500 kcal (~0.5 kg/week gain)

4. Macro Split

Based on goal, the daily calories are split:

  • Lose Weight: 40% protein / 30% carbs / 30% fat (high protein preserves muscle)
  • Maintain: 30% protein / 40% carbs / 30% fat (balanced)
  • Build Muscle: 30% protein / 45% carbs / 25% fat (higher carbs fuel training)
  • Gain Weight: 25% protein / 50% carbs / 25% fat (calorie-dense)

Protein per gram = 4 kcal, Carbs = 4 kcal, Fat = 9 kcal.

💡 Nutrition Principles

  • Protein is king 1.6-2.2 g/kg supports muscle and keeps you full. Highest priority macro.
  • Fiber 25-35 g/day Whole grains, vegetables, fruit. Aids digestion and satiety.
  • Eat whole foods 80% of the time Leave 20% for treats. Perfection isn't sustainable.
  • Don't drink your calories Soda, juice, and fancy coffees add 200-800 kcal without satiety.
  • Sleep 7-9 hours Poor sleep raises hunger hormones (ghrelin) and drops leptin you'll eat more.
  • Weigh yourself weekly, not daily Daily weight fluctuates 1-2 kg from water and food.
  • Track for 2 weeks Learn what portions actually look like, then stop tracking if you prefer.
  • Consistency > perfection 80% adherence for a year beats 100% for a month.
