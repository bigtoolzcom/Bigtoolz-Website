🌅 Drink 1 glass of water right after waking you're dehydrated after 7-8 hours of sleep.
🍽 Drink a glass 30 min before each meal aids digestion and reduces overeating.

💧 What is a Water Intake Calculator?

A Water Intake Calculator estimates how much water you should drink each day based on your body weight, activity level, climate, age, and gender. The popular "8 glasses a day" rule is a rough guideline but real needs vary widely. A 100kg athlete in hot weather needs far more than a 50kg office worker in a temperate climate.

Proper hydration affects almost everything: energy levels, brain function, digestion, skin health, kidney function, and even mood. Even mild dehydration (1-2% of body weight) reduces cognitive performance and physical endurance. This calculator gives you a personalized daily target, plus a tracker to actually hit it.

📊 The Formula We Use

Our calculator uses a simplified but accurate model based on multiple peer-reviewed sources:

  • Base need: 30-35 ml per kg of body weight (for adults)
  • Activity: +0.3L (light) to +1L (athlete) per day
  • Climate: +0-0.5L depending on temperature
  • Age: Children need slightly more per kg, seniors slightly less
  • Pregnancy: +0.3L (pregnant), +0.7L (breastfeeding)

Example: 70kg adult, light activity, temperate climate = 70 × 35ml = 2.1L base + 0.3L activity = 2.4L/day (~9-10 glasses).

💡 Why Hydration Matters

Brain Function

Your brain is ~75% water. Mild dehydration (1-2%) impairs concentration, short-term memory, and mood. Studies show even 1% dehydration reduces cognitive performance measurably.

Physical Performance

Muscles are ~75% water. Losing 2% of body weight in fluid reduces strength and endurance by 10-20%. Athletes should hydrate before, during, and after training.

Digestion & Metabolism

Water is essential for breaking down food and absorbing nutrients. It also helps prevent constipation and supports kidney function.

Skin & Joints

Hydrated skin looks plumper and more elastic. Water also lubricates joints and cushions organs.

Weight Management

Drinking water before meals can reduce calorie intake. Studies show people who drink 500ml before meals lose more weight than those who don't.
