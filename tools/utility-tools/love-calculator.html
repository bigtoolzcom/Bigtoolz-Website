❤️ What is a Love Calculator?

A Love Calculator is a fun online tool that generates a love percentage between two names. You enter your name and your partner's (or crush's) name, click the button, and instantly get a score from 0% to 100% along with a cute message. It's one of the most popular internet tools great for sharing with friends, on social media, or as a lighthearted icebreaker.

Important: The love calculator is purely for entertainment. It's not based on psychology, astrology, or any real science. Real relationships are built on trust, communication, respect, and shared values not on a random percentage. Use it to have fun, not to make decisions.

🎯 What Makes This Calculator Special?

Consistent Results

Most love calculators give you a random number each time. Ours uses a deterministic hash algorithm the same pair of names always produces the exact same result. So if you get 85% today, you'll get 85% tomorrow. This makes it screenshot-friendly and shareable.

100% Private

All calculations happen locally in your browser. Your names are never sent to any server. No tracking, no logging, no third-party analytics on your inputs. What you type stays on your device.

Easy Sharing

One-click share buttons for WhatsApp, Twitter, and Facebook let you brag about your score (or laugh at a low one) with friends instantly. The Copy button gives you a clean text format for any messaging app.
