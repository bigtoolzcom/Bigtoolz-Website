🎲 What is a Decision Maker?

A Decision Maker is a tool that picks a random option from your list taking the burden of deciding off your shoulders. Whether it's as simple as yes/no, chicken vs. pizza, or as complex as choosing between 20 options, randomness settles the question fairly and instantly. Perfect for couples, roommates, teams, classroom activities, and anyone stuck in analysis paralysis.

Unlike most online tools, our Decision Maker runs 100% in your browser and uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same source used by security-critical applications. The result is truly fair and impossible to rig.

🎯 The 3 Modes Explained

🤔 Yes / No Mode

The fastest way to a decision a random yes or no. Great for confirming plans, quick calls, or a coin-flip-style answer without any input. Options: Yes, No, Maybe (3-option variant).

🅰️ A / B Mode

Two-option comparison pick between two choices. Perfect for "Should we do A or B?" situations. Just rename the options to match your choices.

📝 Custom List Mode

Enter any list of options and let randomness pick. Ideal for restaurant picks, movie nights, "what to do this weekend?", or any decision with more than two choices.

⚖️ Why Our Random is Fair

Cryptographic Randomness

We use crypto.getRandomValues() the same API used by banks and security software to pick the winner. No pattern, no bias, no predictability.

Weighted Option Available

For situations where probabilities aren't equal, enable weighted picks. Assign a weight to each option and higher weights mean more likely to be chosen.

Independent Picks

Every decision is a fresh random draw. Previous decisions have no effect on future ones you can use it as much as you want without any pattern emerging.
