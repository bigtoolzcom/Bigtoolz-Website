✅ What is a Habit Tracker?

A habit tracker is a simple tool that helps you build new habits by tracking them daily. Every time you do the habit, you mark it done. Over time, you build a visible streak a chain of consecutive days that motivates you to keep going. Whether you want to exercise daily, read more, meditate, drink water, or quit a bad habit, tracking makes you accountable.

Unlike most habit apps that require signup and cloud storage, BigToolz's habit tracker runs 100% in your browser. Your data is saved locally on your device using browser storage nothing is uploaded to any server. You can export JSON backups anytime.

💡 The Science of Habit Building

Don't Break the Chain

The "Seinfeld Strategy" is simple: do the habit daily, mark it on a calendar, and don't break the chain. Each consecutive day makes the chain more valuable and harder to break. It's the single most effective habit technique.

Start Small Really Small

Stanford behavior scientist BJ Fogg recommends "tiny habits": 2 pushups, 1 page of reading, 1 glass of water. Small habits stick because they're easy. Once consistent, scale up.

21/66 Days Myth

The "21 days to form a habit" is a myth. Research by Phillippa Lally at UCL shows habits take 18-254 days, averaging 66 days. Simple habits (drinking water) form faster than complex ones (exercising daily).

Track, Don't Judge

Missing one day is not failure it's data. The goal is to reduce the number of missed days, not eliminate them. Consistency over months beats perfection over weeks.

Habit Stacking

Attach a new habit to an existing one. "After I brush my teeth, I will meditate for 2 minutes." This leverages existing neural pathways to build new ones.
