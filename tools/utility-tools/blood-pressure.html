🩺 What is a Blood Pressure Tracker?

A Blood Pressure Tracker is a personal log for recording your blood pressure readings over time. Since hypertension (high BP) is often asymptomatic, regular tracking is one of the most important things you can do for your heart health. It reveals trends that a single doctor's-office reading can't and gives your doctor a much clearer picture.

Our tracker stores readings in your browser never uploaded to a server. It shows your AHA (American Heart Association) category instantly, charts your trend over time, calculates averages, and lets you export a CSV for doctor visits.

⚠️
Medical Disclaimer This tool is for personal logging only not medical advice. If your readings are consistently in Stage 2 (140/90+) or Crisis (180/120+), seek medical help immediately. Never change medications without your doctor's guidance.

💡 Best Practices for Measuring

  • Same time daily. Measure in the morning (before coffee/meds) and evening (before dinner). Consistency reveals trends.
  • Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring rushing inflates readings.
  • Feet flat on floor, back supported. Crossed legs or slouching raise BP.
  • Arm at heart level. Support your forearm on a table. Don't hold it up.
  • No caffeine, exercise, or smoking for 30 minutes before.
  • Empty your bladder first a full bladder can add 10-15 mmHg.
  • Take 2-3 readings 1 minute apart. Average them.
  • Use a validated monitor. Wrist monitors are less accurate than upper-arm cuffs.
  • Don't talk during measurement talking adds up to 15 mmHg.
  • Log consistently, even if readings are "good" the trend matters more than any single number.

🔒 Privacy

All readings are stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is uploaded. No account, no tracking. But remember: clearing browser data will erase your log. Use Export CSV regularly for backup and doctor sharing.
