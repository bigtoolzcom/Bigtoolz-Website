🎡 What is Wheel of Names?

Wheel of Names is a spinning wheel random picker a colorful digital version of the classic spinning wheel you'd see at carnivals. Enter a list of names, click spin, and the wheel reveals a random winner. It's the most fun way to pick a winner for giveaways, classrooms, team meetings, game nights, contests, and any situation where you need a fair, visible random selection.

Unlike most online wheels, ours runs 100% in your browser and uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same source used for security-critical randomness. The winner is chosen before the spin even starts, then the wheel animates to land on that segment. This guarantees the result is never biased by the spin physics.

🎯 Why Ours is Fair

Winner Chosen Before Spin

Most wheel pickers animate a spin and stop at a predetermined point with the physics deciding the winner. Ours flips it: we pick the winner with crypto randomness first, then animate the wheel to land there. The physics never influences the outcome.

Cryptographic Randomness

We use crypto.getRandomValues() the same API banks and security software use. No pattern, no predictability, no bias toward any segment.

Equal Segments

Every name gets an equal arc on the wheel. No segment is larger or smaller. The visual is a 1:1 match to the probability.
