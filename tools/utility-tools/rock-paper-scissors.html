✊ What is Rock Paper Scissors?

Rock Paper Scissors (RPS) is one of the most universally known hand games, played for centuries to settle disputes, make decisions, or just for fun. Two players simultaneously form one of three shapes with their hands Rock (fist), Paper (open palm), or Scissors (two fingers) and the winner is decided by a simple rule set.

Our free online version pits you against the computer. Choose your move, watch the countdown, and see who wins in real-time. Track your wins/losses across sessions, play best-of-X matches, and enjoy a clean, fast, distraction-free game.

🎯 The Rules

Rock Paper Scissors has the simplest rules of any game:

✊✌️
Rock beats Scissors
Rock smashes Scissors
✌️✋
Scissors beats Paper
Scissors cuts Paper
✋✊
Paper beats Rock
Paper covers Rock

Same choices = draw. In a best-of-X match, first player to win the majority of rounds wins the match.

🎓 Rock Paper Scissors Strategy

Here's the truth: against a truly random computer opponent, no strategy guarantees winning. Every move has exactly a 33.33% chance of being played. But that hasn't stopped mathematicians from studying the game for decades.

Theoretical Facts

  • Mixed Strategy Nash Equilibrium the mathematically optimal play is 1/3 Rock, 1/3 Paper, 1/3 Scissors randomly.
  • Winning patterns against humans humans tend to favor Rock (35.6%), then Paper (35%), then Scissors (29.6%).
  • Rock is underrated despite being seen as "boring," Rock has the highest win rate in casual play.
  • Losers switch after a loss, players switch moves 70% of the time.
  • Repeat winners winners often repeat their winning move, expecting the opponent to think they won't.

Simple Tips

  • Don't repeat your last losing move the computer assumes you won't.
  • Against humans, "always Rock" actually wins 35.6% of the time.
  • If the opponent just won with Paper, they'll likely switch to Scissors. Counter with Rock.
  • Random is best. Any pattern will eventually be exploited.
