🌍 What is a World Clock?

A world clock shows the current time in multiple cities around the world simultaneously. Unlike a regular clock that only shows your local time, a world clock lets you track time in other time zones instantly. It's essential for remote teams, international businesses, travelers, and anyone scheduling calls across time zones.

Our world clock runs 100% in your browser and uses your device's built-in IANA timezone database the same database that powers your phone's clock and every major calendar app. Your city preferences are stored locally on your device, never uploaded to any server.
