⏱ What is an Online Stopwatch?

An online stopwatch is a digital timer that measures elapsed time with high precision. Unlike physical stopwatches, our browser-based version tracks time to the millisecond (1/1000th of a second) using high-resolution timing APIs. It works on any device desktop, tablet, or phone without any download or installation.

Perfect for workouts, interval training, cooking, scientific experiments, presentations, meetings, coding sprints, or timing anything where you need accuracy and lap tracking.

🎯 Common Use Cases

🏃 Fitness & Workouts

Time HIIT intervals, track running splits, or measure rest periods between sets. The lap feature is perfect for interval training hit Lap at each transition and compare performance across rounds.

🍳 Cooking & Baking

Time multiple dishes, track resting periods, or measure precise cooking times. The large display is readable from across the kitchen, and sounds alert you without watching the screen.

🔬 Scientific Experiments

Measure reaction times, reaction rates, or event durations. Millisecond precision is sufficient for most lab work, and the CSV export makes data analysis easy.

📊 Presentations & Meetings

Keep each agenda item on track. Hit Lap at each transition to see how long each section took. The print-friendly output makes a great time audit for future meetings.

💻 Productivity & Focus

Time focused work sessions, pomodoro-style sprints, or coding challenges. Record a lap each time you switch tasks to see where your time goes.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use keyboard shortcuts. In workouts, keys are faster than tapping Space to pause, L to lap.
  • Enable Wake Lock for long sessions so your phone screen doesn't turn off.
  • Copy the times before resetting clipboard keeps them even after clearing.
  • Export CSV for serious analysis. Open in Excel or Google Sheets to graph your splits.
  • Print for records the print layout removes all UI clutter, showing only laps and total time.
  • Switch tabs freely. The stopwatch runs in the background using high-precision timers.
