🎯 What is an Aim Trainer?

An Aim Trainer is a tool that tests and improves your mouse accuracy and reaction speed. You click on targets that appear at random locations on the screen your score depends on how many you hit, how accurate you are, and how fast you react.

Aim trainers are used extensively by FPS (First-Person Shooter) players CS:GO, Valorant, Apex Legends, Overwatch, Call of Duty, and Rainbow Six Siege. Even a few minutes of daily aim training can improve in-game performance. Non-gamers can also use aim trainers to improve general mouse control and reaction time.

🎮 The 3 Game Modes

⏱ Timed (30 seconds)

You have 30 seconds. Hit as many targets as possible. Your score = hits. Best mode for measuring raw speed and click accuracy. Works well for benchmarking progress.

🎯 Precision (20 targets)

20 targets total. Each miss subtracts from your final score. Rewards accuracy over speed. Great for practicing careful, deliberate aiming.

♾️ Endurance (until 3 misses)

No time limit. Play until you miss 3 times. Score = number of hits before 3 misses. Tests consistency the hardest mode for most players.

💡 Tips to Improve Your Aim

  • Use a gaming mouse. Higher DPI (800-1600) gives finer control than a standard office mouse.
  • Lower your sensitivity. Pro players use 400-800 DPI with 2-4 in-game sensitivity. Lower sensitivity = more precision.
  • Use a large mousepad. More space = bigger arm movements = more consistent aim.
  • Practice daily. 10-15 minutes a day beats 2 hours once a week.
  • Focus on accuracy first. Speed follows naturally. Rushing leads to bad habits.
  • Warm up before gaming. 5 minutes of aim training before ranked matches noticeably helps.
  • Sit comfortably. Wrist and arm position matter use a neutral grip and rest your forearm.
  • Fix your setup. 144Hz+ monitor, wired mouse, and a stable surface give you a measurable edge.
  • Don't chase score. Focus on smooth tracking and precise clicks. Score follows.

🖱️ Mouse Settings That Matter

  • DPI: 800 is a common sweet spot. Higher DPI isn't automatically better.
  • Polling rate: 1000 Hz reduces input lag vs. 125 Hz.
  • Acceleration: Turn OFF pointer acceleration for consistent muscle memory.
  • Windows sensitivity: Keep at 6/11 (middle) never higher.
  • Raw input: Enable in games whenever possible.
