🎰 What is a Lottery Number Generator?

A Lottery Number Generator creates random numbers for lottery games like Powerball, Mega Millions, EuroMillions, and national lotteries. Instead of manually picking numbers which introduces psychological bias (birthdays, patterns, lucky numbers) a generator uses cryptographically secure randomness to produce truly unpredictable picks.

Our tool uses the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same cryptographic RNG your browser uses for HTTPS not the predictable Math.random() that many free tools use. Numbers never repeat within a ticket, and bonus balls are drawn from their own separate pools, matching real lottery mechanics.

🎯 Supported Lottery Formats

🎲 6/49

Pick 6 numbers from 1-49. Used by Canada Lotto 6/49, UK National Lottery, and many national lotteries worldwide. Simple, classic format.

🔴 Powerball (US)

Pick 5 numbers from 1-69 plus 1 Powerball from 1-26. The Powerball is drawn from a separate pool, so it can match any of the main numbers. Jackpot odds: 1 in 292.2 million.

🟡 Mega Millions (US)

Pick 5 numbers from 1-70 plus 1 Mega Ball from 1-25. Similar structure to Powerball. Jackpot odds: 1 in 302.6 million.

🔵 EuroMillions

Pick 5 numbers from 1-50 plus 2 Lucky Stars from 1-12. Pan-European game with two bonus balls. Jackpot odds: 1 in 139.8 million.

⚙️ Custom

Set your own rules how many numbers, min/max range, and optional bonus ball. Perfect for regional lotteries or custom games.

💡 Smart Lottery Tips

Avoid Popular Numbers

Many players pick birthdays (1-31) and patterns. If you win with these numbers, you share the jackpot with many others. Random numbers from higher ranges (32-49) reduce shared-win probability.

Skip "Lucky" Numbers

7, 11, 13, 21, and other "lucky" numbers are picked more often. Random generation avoids this clustering automatically.

Buy More Tickets = Better Odds

No strategy improves per-ticket odds, but 10 tickets vs 1 ticket = 10x the chance. Our bulk feature helps you generate varied tickets quickly.

Never Play More Than You Can Afford

The lottery is entertainment, not an investment strategy. Set a monthly budget and stick to it. Expected value is negative for every major lottery.

Syndicates Help

Joining a lottery syndicate (group of players pooling tickets) massively increases win probability, though the jackpot is shared. Popular among office groups and families.
