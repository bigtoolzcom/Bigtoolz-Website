🪙 What is a Virtual Coin Flip?

A virtual coin flip is a digital version of the classic coin toss the simplest and most trusted way to make a random binary decision. Heads or tails, yes or no, do it or don't. It's been used for centuries in sports (cricket, football, American football) to decide who starts, and it's still the fastest way to settle arguments fairly.

Unlike most online coin flips, ours runs 100% in your browser and uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same source used by banks and security software. Every flip is genuinely 50/50, impossible to rig, and works offline.

🎯 Why Our Coin Flip is Fair

Cryptographic Randomness

We use crypto.getRandomValues() the same API used in banking, cryptography, and security systems. The result is genuinely unpredictable, unlike Math.random() which has predictable patterns.

Truly 50/50

Each side has exactly 50% chance every flip. There's no bias toward heads or tails, and the result is independent of previous flips the coin never "remembers".

Verifiable Stats

We track every flip. After many flips, you'll see the distribution approach 50/50 a mathematical proof that our randomness is fair. Reset stats anytime to start fresh.
