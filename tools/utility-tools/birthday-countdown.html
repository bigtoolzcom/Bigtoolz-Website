🎂 What is a Birthday Countdown?

A birthday countdown is a live clock that shows the exact time remaining until your next birthday. It updates every second, so you can watch the days, hours, minutes and seconds tick down as your special day approaches. Perfect for building excitement, planning parties, or simply satisfying curiosity.

Our free birthday countdown goes beyond just showing days. It also calculates the age you're turning, your zodiac sign, the day of the week your birthday falls on, and even generates a birthday wish message you can copy and send. Save multiple birthdays for family and friends everything persists in your browser.

🎉 Fun Birthday Facts

  • Same birthday? There's a 50% chance at least two people share a birthday in a group of only 23 people.
  • Most common birthday: September 9 (US data) likely due to New Year's conceptions.
  • Rarest birthday: February 29 only 1 in 1,461 people are leap-year babies.
  • Most popular birth month: August, followed by July and September.
  • Birthday paradox: In any random group of 23+ people, there's a >50% chance two share a birthday.
  • Chinese zodiac: Repeats every 12 years, based on year of birth.

💡 Birthday Party Planning Tips

  • Send invites 3-4 weeks ahead for weekend parties, 6-8 weeks for destination events.
  • Pick a date wisely. Saturday evenings usually work best for adults; weekend afternoons for kids.
  • Check the day of week using this tool a Tuesday birthday might be celebrated on the nearest weekend.
  • Plan for weather if outdoors always have an indoor backup.
  • Keep the guest list tight. Fewer, closer people always make better parties than huge crowds.
  • Order cake 1 week in advance for custom designs; 2 days for standard cakes.
