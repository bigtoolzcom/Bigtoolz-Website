🍽 What is a Recipe Calorie Calculator?

A recipe calorie calculator helps you figure out the total calories and macros of a recipe by adding up the nutritional values of each ingredient, then dividing by the number of servings. It's essential for anyone tracking calories, meal prepping, or cooking for a specific diet plan.

Our free recipe calorie calculator uses a 50+ food database covering common ingredients chicken, rice, eggs, paneer, vegetables, oils, spices, and more. Just type the ingredient, pick from suggestions, and set quantity. Everything is computed instantly in your browser no signup, no tracking.

📐 How It Works

The math is simple but accurate:

  • Per ingredient: (Quantity × Nutrition per 100g) ÷ 100
  • Total recipe: Sum of all ingredient values
  • Per serving: Total ÷ Servings

Macronutrients have fixed calorie values:

  • Protein: 4 calories per gram
  • Carbohydrates: 4 calories per gram
  • Fat: 9 calories per gram
  • Alcohol: 7 calories per gram (not tracked here)

🎯 Pro Tips for Accuracy

  • Weigh raw, not cooked. 100g raw rice becomes ~300g cooked but has the same calories. Always weigh before cooking.
  • Add cooking oils separately. 1 tbsp oil = ~120 calories. Frying can add 200-500 calories versus steaming.
  • Include everything. Salt, spices, and herbs are near-zero calories but sauces, cheese, and dressings add up fast.
  • Use a kitchen scale. Cup and spoon estimates can be off by 20-40%. Digital scale = accuracy.
  • Account for evaporation. If your recipe reduces (gravy, curry), the final weight is less than the sum of ingredients but calories stay the same.
  • Double-check portions. Weigh your final cooked dish, then divide by the number of servings. This is more accurate than "one serving = 1/4 recipe".
