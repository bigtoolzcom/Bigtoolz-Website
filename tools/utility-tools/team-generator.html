👥 What is a Random Team Generator?

A Random Team Generator takes a list of names and splits them into balanced, randomly-assigned teams. It's the digital version of drawing straws but faster, fairer, and impossible to rig. Perfect for sports, classrooms, escape rooms, hackathons, workshops, team-building events, and any situation where you need to form groups without bias.

Unlike most online tools, our generator runs 100% in your browser and uses the browser's cryptographically secure random number generator (crypto.getRandomValues()) the same source used for security-critical randomness. Names never leave your device, and every split is mathematically unbiased.

📐 By Team Count vs By Team Size

By Team Count

Choose how many teams you want (2-20). Names are distributed as evenly as possible. Example: 10 names, 3 teams → 4-3-3.

By Team Size

Choose how many people per team (1-10). The tool creates as many full teams as possible, then puts the remainder in one final team. Example: 11 names, 4 per team → 4-4-3.

⚖️ Why Our Split is Fair

Cryptographic Shuffle

We use the Fisher-Yates shuffle algorithm with crypto.getRandomValues() the gold standard for fair shuffling. Every permutation is equally likely.

Balanced Distribution

After shuffling, names are dealt round-robin into teams, so no team is systematically larger. Sizes differ by at most 1 person.

Transparent

Every list is visible. Every shuffle is independent regenerate gives a genuinely different split each time.
