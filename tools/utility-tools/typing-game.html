⌨️ What is a Typing Speed Test?

A Typing Speed Test measures how fast and accurately you type usually in Words Per Minute (WPM). Typing speed is one of the most underrated professional skills. Programmers, writers, customer support staff, data entry operators, and journalists all rely on fast, accurate typing to be productive.

Our typing test uses real English text and measures your WPM, accuracy, correct characters, and mistakes in real time. Choose from 30, 60, or 120 seconds, and 3 difficulty levels. Your personal best is saved automatically.

📐 How WPM is Calculated

The industry-standard formula for WPM is:

  • WPM = (Correct characters / 5) / Minutes elapsed
  • The "5" represents the average word length in English (~5 characters including space)
  • Only correct characters count toward your WPM
  • Incorrect characters reduce accuracy but don't add to WPM

Example

If you type 300 correct characters in 60 seconds: (300 / 5) / 1 = 60 WPM.

💡 Tips to Improve Your Typing Speed

  • Learn touch typing. Use all 10 fingers and never look at the keyboard. This alone can double your speed.
  • Home row position. Rest fingers on ASDF (left hand) and JKL; (right hand). Return to this position after every key.
  • Accuracy first, speed second. Don't rush. Slow, accurate typing builds muscle memory speed follows naturally.
  • Practice daily. 15-20 minutes a day is more effective than 2 hours once a week.
  • Use rhythm, not bursts. Steady typing beats fast-then-slow patterns.
  • Don't look at the keyboard. Even when you make mistakes trust your fingers. Looking breaks muscle memory.
  • Take breaks. Typing fatigue reduces accuracy. Rest for 5 minutes every 30 minutes.
  • Track progress. Measure your WPM weekly to see improvements.
