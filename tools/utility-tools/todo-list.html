📋 What is a To-Do List?

A to-do list is a simple but powerful productivity tool. By writing down your tasks and marking them off as you complete them you free your mind from remembering everything. Research shows that to-do lists reduce stress, improve focus, and boost completion rates by up to 40%.

Unlike most online task managers, BigToolz's to-do list runs 100% in your browser. Your tasks are saved locally on your device using the browser's built-in storage nothing is uploaded to any server. No signup, no ads, no tracking. You can export or delete your data anytime.

🎯 Features Explained

Priorities

Each task has a priority Low, Medium, or High. High priority tasks appear with a red left border, so you can spot them instantly. Use priority to decide what to work on first.

Due Dates

Add a due date to each task. Tasks due today show a yellow badge, and overdue tasks turn red. Perfect for deadlines and daily planning.

Categories

Tag tasks with categories like "Work", "Personal", "Shopping" then filter by category using the search box. Great for separating life and work tasks.

Filters

Filter tabs let you focus on All, Active, Completed, Today, or Overdue tasks. Each tab shows a live count so you always know how many tasks are in each bucket.

Progress Tracking

A progress bar shows your completion percentage. There's a psychological reward in watching it grow it motivates you to finish more tasks.

💡 To-Do List Best Practices

  • Keep it small. 5-10 tasks per day is realistic. 50 is overwhelming.
  • Use action verbs. "Call client" beats "Client". Specific tasks get done.
  • Set priorities honestly. Not everything is High. Reserve High for truly urgent work.
  • Add due dates. A task without a deadline tends to slip forever.
  • Review daily. Spend 2 minutes each morning planning, 2 minutes each evening reflecting.
  • Break big tasks. "Launch website" → "Buy domain", "Design homepage", etc.
  • Celebrate completions. The progress bar and stats are there to motivate you.
