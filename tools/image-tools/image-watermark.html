💧 What is Image Watermarking?

Image watermarking means adding a visible mark text or logo to a photo to claim ownership, brand it, or protect it from unauthorized use. Watermarks are commonly used by photographers, designers, bloggers, real estate agents, and businesses sharing images online. A subtle watermark deters theft without ruining the photo; a bold one makes your brand unmissable.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes images 100% in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your photos never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

📐 Best Practices for Watermarks

Opacity

20-30%: Subtle perfect for portfolio photos where the image matters most. 40-60%: Balanced clearly visible but not distracting. 70-100%: Bold great for stock photos, real estate, previews.

Position

Bottom-right corner: The most common and least distracting placement. Center: Maximum security prevents cropping to remove. Tiled: Best for high-theft risk (stock previews, event photos).

Text Watermark vs Logo Watermark

Text is fast, simple, and easy to read. Use for copyright notices (© Name Year), URLs, or short brand names. Logo is more professional and instantly recognizable use PNG with transparent background for best results.

Font Choice

Sans-serif (Inter, Arial) modern, clean, great for most uses. Serif (Georgia, Times) classic, elegant, good for photographers. Monospace technical, distinctive, good for developer portfolios.
