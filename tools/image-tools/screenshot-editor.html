📸 What is a Screenshot Editor?

A screenshot editor lets you annotate and enhance screenshots adding arrows to point at things, rectangles to highlight areas, text to explain, highlighter for emphasis, and blur to hide sensitive info. It's an essential tool for bug reports, tutorials, documentation, Slack messages, help desk tickets, and product demos.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes screenshots 100% in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. Your screenshots never uploaded to any server complete privacy, especially important for screenshots with sensitive data.

🎯 The 7 Tools Explained

↗ Arrow

Point at anything with a clear arrow. Best for bug reports ("click here") and tutorials ("notice this button"). Draw from tail to tip.

▭ Rectangle

Draw a box around a specific area. Perfect for highlighting form fields, buttons, or regions in a screenshot.

◯ Circle / Ellipse

Circular or oval highlight. More attention-grabbing than a rectangle, great for logos, avatars, or small UI elements.

╱ Line

Draw straight lines. Useful for connecting related elements or creating visual separators.

✎ Freehand Pen

Draw anything by hand. Circle things quickly or underline text with natural curves.

T Text

Add text labels. Click anywhere on the screenshot and type. Great for numbered steps or explanations.

▬ Highlighter

Translucent marker highlights without hiding. Great for emphasizing text or drawing attention to specific rows.

◐ Blur / Pixelate

Hide sensitive information. Drag over passwords, emails, names, or account numbers. Much safer than blacking out the actual pixels are mixed beyond recovery.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Use red (#ef4444) for arrows maximum visibility on any background.
  • Yellow highlighter (#fbbf24) on white backgrounds simulates a real marker.
  • Ctrl+Z to undo 20-step history maintained until page reload.
  • Pick the right tool for one thing, arrow; for many, numbered text.
  • Blur before sharing always censor passwords, tokens, and personal info.
  • Copy to clipboard Ctrl+V right into Slack, email, or docs.
