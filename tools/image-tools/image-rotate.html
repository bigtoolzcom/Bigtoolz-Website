🔁 What is Image Rotate?

Image Rotate is a free online tool that turns your images by any angle from quick 90° adjustments to precise custom angles like 15°, 45°, or 172°. Whether you need to fix a sideways photo, correct a scan, or create dynamic design compositions, rotation is one of the most common editing tasks.

🎯 When to Use Which Rotation

90° Rotations (Lossless)

Common for fixing sideways photos from phone cameras or scanners. 90° rotations are completely lossless every pixel remains identical, only its position changes.

180° Rotation (Lossless)

Flips the image upside-down. Often used to correct photos that were saved upside down, or for creative compositions. Also lossless.

Custom Angles (Lossy)

Non-90° angles like 45°, 15°, or 172° require re-sampling every pixel moves to a fractional position and gets recalculated. Quality remains high (especially at 92%+ quality) but isn't perfectly identical. Best for artistic angles, tilted designs, or straightening crooked photos.
