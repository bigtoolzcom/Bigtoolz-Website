🔃 What is Image Flip?

Image Flip is a free online tool that mirrors your images along a horizontal or vertical axis. Horizontal flip creates a left-to-right mirror (like looking in a mirror). Vertical flip turns the image upside down. Flipping is a lossless transformation no pixels are lost or re-sampled, so quality remains exactly the same as the original.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes images 100% in your browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your images never leave your device complete privacy, instant results, no uploads.

🎯 When to Use Each Flip Mode

Horizontal Flip (Mirror)

Use when you need to reverse the left-right orientation. Common cases: fixing selfie photos (front camera mirrors), correcting text that's backwards, creating symmetrical designs, or matching a subject's gaze direction to a layout.

Vertical Flip (Upside Down)

Use when the image is upside down or you need to reverse the top-bottom orientation. Common cases: fixing scanned documents that are inverted, creative reflections, or artistic compositions.

Both (180° Rotation)

Flipping both axes is equivalent to rotating the image 180°. Use this when you need a full rotation but "as if looking at the back of the photo" great for icons, playing cards, and symmetrical patterns.
