▦ What is a QR Code?

A QR code (Quick Response code) is a 2D barcode that stores information URLs, text, phone numbers, WiFi credentials, or contact details. When scanned by a smartphone camera, the encoded data is instantly recognized and acted upon. QR codes have become essential for contactless menus, WiFi sharing, business cards, marketing campaigns, and payment systems.

Unlike most online QR generators, BigToolz creates static QR codes the data is encoded directly into the pattern with no redirects. Your QR code never expires, never breaks, and isn't tracked. And all generation happens 100% in your browser critical for privacy when encoding WiFi passwords or personal data.

⚡ Error Correction Levels Explained

Low (7% recovery)

Lowest error correction allows more data. Best for QR codes printed on clean surfaces and scanned in ideal conditions. Not recommended for QR codes with logos.

Medium (15% recovery)

Balanced error correction. Good default for most uses URLs, text, business cards, marketing materials. Handles minor damage and glare.

Quartile (25% recovery)

Higher error correction good for logos and small damage. Use when the QR code will be partially covered or printed on textured surfaces.

High (30% recovery)

Maximum error correction recommended for logos covering up to 25% of the QR code. Also great for outdoor environments, stickers, and industrial use.

💡 QR Code Best Practices

  • High contrast dark foreground on light background scans best. Avoid low-contrast colors.
  • Quiet zone leave at least 4 modules of white space around the QR code.
  • Size matters minimum 2×2 cm for close-range scanning, larger for distance.
  • Test before printing scan with 3+ different phones to verify.
  • Logo placement keep logos ≤25% of QR area with error correction H.
  • Short URLs shorter URLs = simpler QR code = easier to scan.
  • SVG for print vector format scales infinitely without quality loss.
