📌 What is Image Compressor?

Image Compressor is a free online tool that reduces the file size of your images without visible quality loss. It supports JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP and GIF formats, and outputs in JPEG, WebP or PNG. Whether you're optimizing images for a website, preparing photos for email, or saving storage space this tool gets the job done in seconds.

Unlike most online image compressors, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser. That means your images are never uploaded to any server. Everything happens locally on your device, so you get complete privacy and instant results without waiting for uploads.

🎯 Best Settings for Common Use Cases

For Website Images

Use WebP format, quality 75-80%, max width 1920px. This gives excellent quality with 60-80% smaller files perfect for faster page loads and better SEO.

For Email Attachments

Use JPEG format, quality 60-70%, max width 1280px. Great balance between size (under 1MB usually) and readability.

For Social Media

Use JPEG or WebP, quality 85%, max dimension 1080px. Instagram, Facebook, and Twitter all prefer smaller files.

For Print or Archives

Use PNG format, quality 100% (PNG is lossless). Note: PNG files are usually larger than JPEG.
