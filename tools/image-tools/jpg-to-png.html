🔄 What is JPG to PNG Conversion?

JPG to PNG conversion transforms images from JPG (also called JPEG) format to PNG format. Both are common image formats, but they serve different purposes. JPG is great for photographs and produces small files, while PNG is preferred for graphics, logos, and images that need transparency or lossless quality.

🎯 When Should You Convert JPG to PNG?

1. You Need Transparency

If you're designing a logo, overlay, or graphic that needs a transparent background, PNG is your only option. Use our transparency toggle to auto-remove white backgrounds.

2. You're Editing Repeatedly

Every time you save a JPG, some quality is lost. Converting to PNG first means you can edit and re-save without further quality degradation.

3. You Need Maximum Quality

For sharp graphics, screenshots, or text-heavy images, PNG preserves every pixel without the artifacts JPG introduces.

4. You're Preparing Print Files

Print shops often prefer PNG for maximum quality. Convert your JPGs to PNG before sending to print.
