✂️ What is Image Crop?

Image Crop is a free online tool that lets you trim, trim away, or cut portions of an image to keep only the part you want. Whether you need a square profile picture, a 16:9 video thumbnail, or you want to remove distracting elements from a photo, cropping is one of the most common image editing tasks and our tool makes it fast and easy.

Unlike most online croppers, BigToolz processes images 100% in your browser. Your images never uploaded to any server the entire crop happens on your device using HTML5 Canvas. Complete privacy, instant results, no signup.

🎯 When to Crop vs Resize

Crop when:

You want to remove parts of an image trimming background, focusing on a subject, or matching a specific aspect ratio. Cropping reduces the visible area and dimensions.

Resize when:

You want to change the overall dimensions without removing content. Resizing scales the entire image up or down. Check our Image Resizer.

Best practice:

For social media, crop first, then resize. Crop to the right aspect ratio, then scale to the exact pixel dimension the platform requires.
