💨 What is Image Blur?

Image Blur applies a soft, out-of-focus effect to your images. The most common type is Gaussian blur a smooth, natural-looking blur based on a mathematical Gaussian distribution. It's the same blur used by Photoshop, GIMP, and CSS filter: blur().

Unlike most online blur tools, BigToolz processes images 100% in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, no uploads.

🎯 When to Use Blur

🔒 Privacy & Censorship

Hide faces, license plates, credit card numbers, addresses, or any personal information. A blur of 20-30px makes text completely unreadable while keeping the image recognizable.

🎨 Design & Backgrounds

Create soft background images for hero sections, cards, or slides. A light blur (5-15px) makes the subject pop while keeping the background visually interesting.

📸 Photo Editing

Soften skin in portraits (2-5px), create a shallow depth-of-field effect, or add a dreamy glow to photos. Combine with brightness adjustments for a "glamour" effect.

🖼️ Creative Effects

Use high blur (40-50px) to create abstract color gradients from photos. Perfect for section dividers, texture backgrounds, or ambient design.

📊 Recommended Blur Strengths

  • 0-3 px: Barely noticeable subtle softening only.
  • 3-8 px: Portrait softening subtle background blur.
  • 8-15 px: Background blur dreamy effect.
  • 15-25 px: Privacy blur for faces and text.
  • 25-40 px: Strong blur content becomes unrecognizable.
  • 40-50 px: Extreme blur abstract color wash only.
