🔃 What is Reverse Text?

Reverse text is a technique that flips text either character-by-character, word-by-word, line-by-line, or a combination. Reversed text is used for hidden messages, puzzles, mirror effects, social media styling, and programming/testing purposes.

Unlike most online reverse text tools, BigToolz processes text 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your text never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, unlimited use.

💡 Reverse Text vs Other Tools

Reverse vs Upside Down

Reverse keeps letters upright but in reverse order. Upside Down flips the actual glyphs upside down (using Unicode). Use our Upside Down Text tool for that effect.

Reverse vs Mirror

In our tool, Mirror means reversing each word individually (right-to-left reading). Some tools call this "backwards text." Both are useful pick based on what you need.

Reverse vs Flip

Flip in our tool reverses word order AND each word's characters giving a double-reversed effect. Great for hidden messages that look completely scrambled.
