What is Morse Code?

Morse code is a method of encoding text as a series of dots (short signals, written as .) and dashes (long signals, written as -). Invented in the 1830s by Samuel Morse and Alfred Vail, it was the foundation of telegraph communication and remains popular today for amateur radio, aviation, emergency signals, and puzzle games.
