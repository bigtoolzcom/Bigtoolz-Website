📝 What is a Word Counter?

A Word Counter is a tool that instantly counts words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs in any text. Writers, bloggers, students, SEO specialists, and social media managers use it daily to hit specific length targets whether it's a 500-word essay, a 280-character tweet, or an SEO meta description.

Unlike most online word counters, BigToolz processes text 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your text never uploaded to any server complete privacy, especially important for confidential documents, drafts, and client work.

📊 What Each Count Means

CountDefinitionExample
WordsSequences separated by spaces"Hello world" = 2 words
CharactersEvery character including spaces"Hi!" = 3 characters
Characters (no space)Characters excluding spaces/tabs"Hi there" = 7 characters
SentencesGroups ending with . ! ?"Hi. Bye!" = 2 sentences

Unique WordsDistinct words (case-insensitive)"cat cat dog" = 2 unique

⚡ Common Length Targets

  • Twitter/X post: 280 characters max
  • Meta description: 150-160 characters
  • Instagram caption: 2,200 characters max
  • Blog post: 1,500-2,500 words (SEO sweet spot)
  • College essay: 500-1,000 words
  • Product description: 150-300 words
  • Press release: 400-600 words
  • Email subject line: 40-60 characters

💡 Best Practices for Writers

  • Count in context write freely first, then count and trim.
  • Watch reading time 3-5 minutes is ideal for blog posts.
  • Check keyword density keep top keywords under 3% to avoid SEO penalties.
  • Unique words matter higher unique ratio = richer vocabulary.
  • Sentence length average 15-20 words per sentence reads best.
  • Save your drafts use the Download button to save stats + text.
