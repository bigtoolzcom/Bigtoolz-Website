🔤 What is a Character Counter?

A Character Counter counts every character in your text letters, digits, symbols, spaces, and punctuation. It's essential for social media posts, SMS messages, meta descriptions, headlines, ads, and product titles where strict length limits determine whether your content gets cut off or performs well.

Unlike most online character counters, BigToolz processes text 100% in your browser using JavaScript. Your text never uploaded to any server complete privacy, especially important for confidential content, drafts, and client work.

💡 Why Character Count Matters

  • Social media over-the-limit content gets truncated or rejected.
  • SMS marketing 160 chars = 1 SMS; longer = multiple charges.
  • SEO meta descriptions and titles have strict limits for full display.
  • Ads Google Ads headlines = 30 chars; descriptions = 90 chars.
  • Product listings Amazon title limit = 200 chars; eBay = 80.
  • Passwords many systems require min/max character length.
  • Database fields some apps have hard character limits.
