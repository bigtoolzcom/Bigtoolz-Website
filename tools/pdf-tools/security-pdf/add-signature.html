✍️ What is Add Signature to PDF?

Add Signature to PDF lets you sign any PDF electronically no printing, scanning, or mailing needed. Create your signature by drawing with a mouse or finger, typing with elegant script fonts, uploading a signature image, or extracting it from an existing PDF. Then drag it into place on any page and download the signed file.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🎨 4 Signature Modes

1. Draw (Freehand)

The most natural option sign with your mouse or finger on the drawing pad. Choose from 4 pen colors (black, blue, red, green). Perfect for authentic-looking signatures with unique strokes.

2. Type (Signature Fonts)

Type your name and pick one of 5 elegant signature fonts: Dancing Script, Great Vibes, Sacramento, Allura, and Pacifico. Great for quick, consistent signatures on multiple documents.

3. Upload Image

Already have a signature image (PNG with transparent background works best)? Upload it and place it on the PDF. Ideal for reusing the same signature across many documents.

4. Extract from PDF

Upload a PDF that already contains your signature (like a previously signed document), and we'll extract the last non-empty page as a reusable signature image.

⚖️ Legal Note

Electronic signatures are legally recognized in most countries: ESIGN Act (US), eIDAS (EU/UK), IT Act 2000 (India), E-SIGN (Australia), and similar laws worldwide. For highest-security legal contracts, some jurisdictions require certified digital signatures (with cryptographic keys). Consult legal counsel for critical documents.
