🕵 What is PDF Anonymization?

PDF anonymization removes or obscures identifying information from a PDF before sharing it. This includes metadata (author name, software used), visible text (emails, phone numbers, addresses), and visual elements (signatures, photos, ID numbers). Anonymized PDFs protect privacy in journalism, legal filings, medical records, HR documents, and research publications.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy.

⚠️ Redaction Method Read This This tool draws opaque blackout overlays on top of the PDF. The output looks identical to full redaction on screen and in print, but the underlying text/image data may still exist inside the file. For legally-verifiable redaction (content permanently removed), use Adobe Acrobat Pro. For most everyday privacy needs (sharing redacted screenshots, hiding contact info), our method is sufficient.

🎯 3 Layers of Anonymization

1. Metadata Cleanup

Every PDF contains hidden properties: author name, original software (Microsoft Word, Pages, etc.), title, subject, and keywords. Our tool clears all 6 standard fields one click.

2. Text Redaction

Search for specific words or patterns (email addresses, phone numbers, names) and black them out automatically. Common PII types are auto-detected. Perfect for resumes, contracts, and customer records.

3. Region Blackout

Click-drag on any page to draw black boxes over sensitive visual areas signatures, ID numbers, photos, watermarks, or any region you need to hide. Works on scanned PDFs too.
