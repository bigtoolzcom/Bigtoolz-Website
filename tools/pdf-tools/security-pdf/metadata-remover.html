🗑 What is PDF Metadata Removal?

PDF metadata removal (also called stripping or sanitizing) clears the hidden information stored inside a PDF the author name, original software, title, keywords, and other properties. This metadata is invisible when you view the PDF, but it's readable by anyone who inspects the file's properties.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🎯 Why Remove PDF Metadata?

🔒 Privacy Protection

Metadata often contains your full name, company/organization, software you use, and sometimes your email or system username. When you share a PDF publicly (resume, proposal, report), this info leaks unintentionally.

🏢 Corporate Compliance

Many organizations require stripping metadata from documents shared outside the company. Metadata can reveal internal team names, software versions, or editing history.

📤 Clean Client Deliverables

Freelancers and agencies typically strip metadata before sending final deliverables it looks more professional and hides the tools used.

⚖️ Legal & Journalism

Journalists, lawyers, and whistleblowers need to remove identifying info before publishing documents. Metadata has unmasked sources in real investigations.

🗑 Removing Embarrassing History

Metadata sometimes shows wrong titles, old author names from templates, or software you don't want to reveal. Cleaning it makes the PDF more presentable.

📋 What Gets Removed

  • Title Document title shown in browsers and readers.
  • Author The person or org attributed to the content.
  • Subject A one-line description of the document.
  • Keywords Comma-separated list for search indexing.
  • Creator The app that created the original content (e.g., Microsoft Word).
  • Producer The app that produced the PDF (e.g., Adobe Acrobat).

🎁 3 Replacement Modes

  • Empty Clear fields completely. Metadata streams remain but are blank.
  • Anonymous Replace every field with the text "Anonymous" looks intentional rather than blank.
  • Custom Replace with your own text (e.g., "BigToolz Client Deliverable").
