✓ What is PDF Digital Signature Verification?

A PDF digital signature is a cryptographic mark that proves who signed the document, when it was signed, and that the content hasn't been altered since. It uses X.509 certificates issued by trusted Certificate Authorities (CAs) like DigiCert, GlobalSign, or self-signed certs for internal use.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy. This tool only analyzes it never modifies your file.

🔍 What This Tool Detects

1. Signature Presence

Scans the PDF's AcroForm (Acrobat form structure) for signature fields. Detects whether the PDF has any digital signatures, and if so, how many.

2. Signer Information

Extracts the signer's name, email (if embedded), organization, and signing location from the signature metadata.

3. Timestamp & Reason

Shows the exact date and time of signing, and the reason for signing if the signer entered one (e.g., "I approve this document").

4. Certificate Details

Extracts X.509 certificate fields subject, issuer, serial number, validity period, and certificate type (self-signed vs CA-issued).

5. Integrity Status

Checks for incremental update markers (byte range changes after signing) to indicate whether the PDF was modified after signature.

🎯 Electronic vs Digital Signatures

Electronic Signature

Any electronic mark of intent a typed name, drawn signature, or click-wrap. Legal in many jurisdictions but easy to forge and doesn't prove integrity.

Digital Signature

Uses public-key cryptography with X.509 certificates. Provides:

  • Authentication proves who signed (via certificate)
  • Integrity proves document wasn't changed
  • Non-repudiation signer cannot deny signing

Digital signatures are the strongest form and are legally recognized in most countries (eSignature laws, eIDAS, ESIGN Act).
