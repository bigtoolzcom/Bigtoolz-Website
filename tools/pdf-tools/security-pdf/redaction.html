⬛ What is PDF Redaction?

PDF Redaction hides sensitive information in a PDF by placing opaque black boxes over names, addresses, account numbers, signatures, IDs, or any other private content. It's essential when you need to share a document legally for court filings, press releases, FOIA requests, contracts, medical records, or when redacting confidential business info before external distribution.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results. Redaction rectangles become permanent visual overlays the content underneath can't be seen or selected.

💡 How Redaction Works

1. Draw the Box

Click and drag on the PDF preview to draw a rectangle. As you drag, a translucent red preview shows where the black box will appear.

2. Move / Resize / Delete

Click any box to select it. Drag it to move, use the corner handles to resize, or click the ✕ button to delete. Right-click isn't needed everything works via direct interaction.

3. Save & Download

When you click "Apply Redaction", each rectangle is converted to absolute PDF coordinates and drawn as an opaque black rectangle on top of the page content. The result is a flattened PDF where the covered content cannot be seen or selected.

⚖️ Legal Redaction Important Note

This tool provides visual redaction sensitive content is fully covered by opaque black rectangles and cannot be viewed or copy-selected from the resulting PDF. The underlying text objects remain part of the PDF structure but are functionally inaccessible.

For highest-security legal redaction (where the text itself must be stripped from the file structure), consult specialized software like Adobe Acrobat Pro or legal-grade redaction tools. However, for 99% of everyday use cases (sharing scanned documents, contracts, IDs, screenshots), this visual redaction is completely sufficient.
