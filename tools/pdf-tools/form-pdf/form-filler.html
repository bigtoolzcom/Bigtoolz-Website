✍️ What is a PDF Form Filler?

A PDF form filler lets you complete fillable PDF forms like job applications, tax returns, insurance forms, contracts, and registrations right in your browser. You click on each field and type your answers. Instead of printing and handwriting the form, you get a clean, digital, shareable PDF that looks professional and is easy to store.

BigToolz's Fill PDF Form tool auto-detects all fields in your PDF using pdf-lib, then renders clickable overlays. Fill them by typing into the side panel or clicking on the page. Everything runs 100% in your browser no uploads, no signup.

📌 Editable vs Flattened Which to Choose?

✏️ Editable (Recommended for drafts)

Keeps the form fields interactive. Anyone with Adobe Acrobat Reader, Foxit, or macOS Preview can still edit the values. Best when:

  • You're sending for review or co-signing
  • The recipient may need to correct values
  • You want to re-fill it later

🔒 Flattened (Recommended for final submission)

Bakes the values into the page like an image. Fields become non-editable the content is locked in place. Best when:

  • You're submitting the final version to an official body
  • You want to prevent accidental edits
  • The recipient has older PDF readers that struggle with form fields

🎯 What Fields Are Supported?

  • Text fields names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, dates.
  • Checkboxes yes/no, agree/disagree toggles.
  • Dropdowns select menus with predefined options.
  • Radio buttons single-choice options from a group.
  • Signature fields filled with your typed name or e-signature.

💡 Tips for Filling PDF Forms

  • Use Tab to move between fields quickly don't click each one.
  • Check dates format some forms require DD/MM/YYYY, others MM/DD/YYYY.
  • Fill required fields first marked with an asterisk or "required" note.
  • Save as editable while working, then flatten for final submission.
  • Keep a blank copy of the original PDF so you can re-fill later.
