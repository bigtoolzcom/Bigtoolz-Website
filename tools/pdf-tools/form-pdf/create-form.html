📝 What is a Fillable PDF Form?

A fillable PDF form contains interactive fields text boxes, checkboxes, dropdown menus, radio buttons, and signature placeholders that anyone can click and type into using a PDF reader. Unlike static PDFs (where you'd need to print and write), fillable forms let users complete, save, and email the document digitally. They're the standard for job applications, tax forms, contracts, surveys, registrations, and official documents.

BigToolz uses pdf-lib to create real AcroForm fields the official PDF standard supported by Adobe Acrobat, Foxit Reader, macOS Preview, and every modern PDF reader. Everything runs 100% in your browser no uploads, no signup, no limits.

🎨 The 5 Field Types Explained

📝 Text Field

The most common field for names, emails, addresses, phone numbers, or any short text. Supports default values, custom font sizes, and required flag.

☑️ Checkbox

A toggle for yes/no or agree/disagree options. Perfect for terms acceptance, opt-ins, and boolean choices.

▼ Dropdown

A select menu with predefined options. Great for country selection, department choice, or any limited list.

◉ Radio Button

A single-choice option from a group. Use for gender, payment method, or satisfaction ratings.

✍️ Signature Placeholder

A reserved area for digital signatures. Users can sign with Adobe's e-signature tool, macOS Preview, or other signing apps.

📐 Understanding PDF Coordinates

PDF uses a bottom-left origin system:

  • (0, 0) = bottom-left corner.
  • X increases going right.
  • Y increases going up.
  • 1 point (pt) = 1/72 inch = 0.353 mm.

Common Page Sizes

  • A4 595 × 842 pt
  • US Letter 612 × 792 pt
  • Legal 612 × 1008 pt

Quick Formula

If you know the distance from the top: Y = Page_Height − Distance_From_Top
