📋 What Makes a Strong Resume?

Structure

  • Contact section name, title, email, phone, location, LinkedIn.
  • Summary 2-4 sentences stating who you are and what you bring.
  • Experience most recent first, with quantifiable achievements.
  • Education degree, institution, year, GPA (if strong).
  • Skills relevant technical and soft skills, keyword-matched to the job.
  • Projects / Certs extra section for portfolio or certification work.

Length

One page for under 10 years of experience. Two pages max for senior roles. Never more recruiters spend 6-7 seconds on initial screening.

Formatting Rules

  • Use standard fonts Helvetica, Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman.
  • Consistent date format "Jan 2020 – Present" everywhere.
  • Bullet points short, action-verb-led, ideally with numbers.
  • No photos for US/UK applications (photos are common in EU/Asia).
  • Save as PDF never .docx unless specifically requested.

💡 Resume Writing Tips

  • Start with action verbs "Led", "Built", "Designed", "Optimized".
  • Quantify results "Reduced load time by 40%", "Grew users from 10k to 100k".
  • Tailor for each job mirror keywords from the job description.
  • Use the ATS-friendly format no tables, no columns, no fancy graphics.
  • Proofread twice spelling errors are the #1 rejection reason.
  • Save both PDF and DOCX some portals prefer one over the other.
