🔗 What is a PDF Link Annotation?

A PDF link annotation is an invisible rectangular area on a page that becomes clickable jumping to a website, another page, or triggering an action. Unlike text URLs (which just look like links), annotations are stored in the PDF structure and recognized by every PDF reader. When you click the rectangle, the URL opens in a browser.

BigToolz uses pdf-lib to create real /Link annotations with /URI actions the exact same format Adobe Acrobat Pro uses. Everything runs 100% in your browser your file never leaves your device.

📐 Understanding PDF Coordinates

PDF uses a bottom-left origin coordinate system:

  • (0, 0) = bottom-left corner of the page.
  • X increases going right.
  • Y increases going up.
  • Units are points (1 pt = 1/72 inch = 0.353 mm).

Common Page Sizes (in points)

  • A4 595 × 842 pt
  • US Letter 612 × 792 pt
  • Legal 612 × 1008 pt
  • A5 420 × 595 pt

Finding Coordinates

If you know the distance from the top: Y = Page_Height − Distance_From_Top. For example, a link 3 inches (216 pt) from the top of a Letter page: Y = 792 − 216 = 576 pt.
