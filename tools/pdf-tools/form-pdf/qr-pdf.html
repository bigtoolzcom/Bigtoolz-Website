▦ What is Add QR Code to PDF?

Adding a QR code to a PDF turns any static document into an interactive experience. Scan it with a phone camera and it can open a website, dial a number, send an email, connect to WiFi, or save a contact all from a printed page. Businesses use QR-in-PDF for marketing brochures, restaurant menus, business cards, event tickets, invoices, product manuals, and contact sheets.

BigToolz's Add QR Code to PDF tool supports 7 content types URL, Text, Email, Phone, SMS, WiFi, vCard powered by qrcode.js and pdf-lib. Everything runs 100% in your browser. No uploads, no signup, no WinAnsi/NaN errors.

🎯 The 7 QR Types Explained

🔗 URL Opens a Website

The most common type. Scan → browser opens the URL. Use for marketing, product pages, social profiles, or any web link.

📝 Text Plain Message

Any text up to ~4,000 characters. Scan → text appears in scanner app. Great for notes, addresses, serial numbers, or short instructions.

✉️ Email Opens Compose Window

Includes recipient, subject, and body. Scan → email app opens with everything pre-filled. Perfect for "Contact us" sections.

📞 Phone Direct Dial

Scan → phone's dialer opens with the number pre-filled. One tap to call. Common on business cards, service vehicles, and restaurant menus.

💬 SMS Pre-filled Message

Phone number + optional message. Scan → SMS app opens ready to send. Used for opt-ins, RSVPs, and quick inquiries.

📶 WiFi Auto-Connect

Encodes network name + password + encryption type. Scan → device connects automatically. Perfect for cafés, hotels, offices. No more typing 20-character passwords.

👤 vCard Digital Contact Card

Encodes name, phone, email, organization, title, and website. Scan → saves contact to phone's address book in one tap. Modern alternative to paper business cards.

📌 Best Practices for PDF QR Codes

  • Minimum size: 80pt (28mm) for phone-camera scanning. Smaller = unreliable.
  • Contrast: black on white works best. Avoid light colors on dark backgrounds.
  • Quiet zone: leave 2-4 module widths of blank space around the QR code.
  • Don't rotate: keep QR codes axis-aligned. Rotated codes still scan but slower.
  • Test print: always scan a test print before mass production.
  • Error correction: our tool uses high error correction (H level = 30% recovery) reliable even with logos or minor damage.
