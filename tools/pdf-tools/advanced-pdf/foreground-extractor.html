🎨 What is PDF Foreground Extraction?

Foreground extraction removes the background from PDF pages and keeps only the meaningful content text, images, and graphics. The result is a transparent PNG with only the foreground, perfect for reusing content in design projects, presentations, or on colored backgrounds.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser using PDF.js. Your PDFs never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, unlimited pages.

🔬 How the Extraction Works

We render each PDF page to a canvas at your chosen resolution, then analyze every pixel:

  • White Remove mode: Pixels where R, G, B all exceed the threshold (default 240) become transparent. Everything else stays opaque.
  • Light Remove mode: Uses a higher threshold (up to 250) to remove cream/tinted backgrounds.
  • Dark Remove mode: Pixels below the threshold become transparent. Use for PDFs with dark backgrounds and light text.

The Softness slider applies a light blur before thresholding, producing smoother anti-aliased edges on text and curves.

💡 Best Practices

  • Start with White Remove covers 95% of PDFs.
  • Adjust threshold: If residual background remains, lower the threshold (try 220). If too much content is removed, raise it (try 245).
  • Crop to content trims empty margins, useful for creating tight icons.
  • Higher resolution for print-quality output, use 3× resolution.
  • Scanned PDFs: May have shading/uneven backgrounds often needs threshold tuning.
  • Keep colors on for colored graphics; turn off for cleaner B&W extraction.
