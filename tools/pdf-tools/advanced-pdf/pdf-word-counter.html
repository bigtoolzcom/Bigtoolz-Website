🔢 What is a PDF Word Counter?

A PDF word counter extracts text from a PDF and counts the words, characters, sentences, and paragraphs. It's essential for writers, students, translators, editors, and anyone who needs to know the length of a document whether you're checking if an essay meets requirements, pricing a translation project, or estimating how long it takes to read.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser using PDF.js. Your PDFs never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, no file size limits.

📊 What Gets Counted

Words

Sequences of characters separated by whitespace or punctuation. Hyphenated words (state-of-the-art) count as one. Numbers, abbreviations, and URLs count as single tokens. This matches Microsoft Word and Google Docs counting.

Characters

Every individual character. We show both with spaces and without spaces useful for Twitter limits, SMS, and social media captions.

Sentences

Split at ., !, and ?. Handles abbreviations like Mr., Dr., U.S. by checking for common patterns.

Paragraphs

Groups of sentences separated by newlines or blank lines. Gives you a sense of the document's structure.

⏱️ Reading Time Calculations

  • Fast reading (200 wpm): Standard adult reading speed. Used by Medium, Kindle, and academic publishers.
  • Slow reading (130 wpm): Careful or technical reading, common for complex documents or non-native speakers.
  • Speaking (150 wpm): Average public speaking rate. Useful for preparing presentations or scripts.
