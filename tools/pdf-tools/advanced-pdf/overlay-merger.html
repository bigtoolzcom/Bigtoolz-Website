📚 What is PDF Overlay Merging?

PDF overlay merging stacks two PDFs on top of each other, page by page. The base PDF stays underneath, and the overlay PDF is drawn on top with adjustable opacity and blend modes. This produces a document with the same number of pages as the base PDF, but with both layers visible.

Unlike regular merge (which appends pages one after another), overlay blends layers together. It's the ideal technique for adding letterheads to invoices, watermarks to contracts, stamps to certificates, or design elements to any existing document.

BigToolz uses pdf-lib to create the overlay and PDF.js to render the preview. Everything runs 100% in your browser your PDFs never leave your device.

🎨 Common Use Cases

📋 Letterheads

Add a company letterhead (as a PDF) on top of invoices, contracts, or reports. Set opacity to 100% and blend mode to Normal for a clean corporate look.

💧 Watermarks

Add "CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", or a logo PDF on top of your document. Lower the opacity to 15-30% for a subtle watermark effect.

🎯 Stamps & Seals

Overlay a digital stamp PDF (like a "PAID" or "APPROVED" seal) on top of documents. Set opacity to 100% for full visibility.

✍️ Background Designs

Overlay a design background PDF (with paper texture or border) on top of plain text documents. Blend mode Multiply produces a print-like effect.

🔒 Security Patterns

Overlay a security pattern (like a tiled logo) at 10% opacity across documents for anti-counterfeiting.
