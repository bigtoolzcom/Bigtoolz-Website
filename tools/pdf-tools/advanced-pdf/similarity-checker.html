📊 What is PDF Similarity Checking?

PDF similarity checking compares the text content of two PDFs and produces a similarity score from 0% (completely different) to 100% (identical). It's used for plagiarism detection, contract comparison, version checking, and content deduplication. If you have a document and want to know if it's a modified version of another or how much overlap exists between two files this is the tool.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser using PDF.js for text extraction and native JavaScript for comparison. Your PDFs never uploaded to any server complete privacy, no file size limits.

🔬 The Algorithms Behind the Score

Cosine Similarity

Treats each PDF as a word-frequency vector in high-dimensional space. Measures the cosine of the angle between the two vectors 1.0 means identical direction (same word distribution), 0 means completely orthogonal (no overlap).

Formula: cos(A,B) = (A · B) / (‖A‖ × ‖B‖)

Jaccard Similarity

Compares the sets of unique words in both PDFs. Ratio of intersection to union 1.0 means both have identical vocabularies, 0 means no shared words.

Formula: J(A,B) = |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B|

Blended Score

Our final score blends both 60% cosine + 40% Jaccard. This gives a balanced result: cosine captures overall word distribution, while Jaccard captures vocabulary overlap.

📏 Interpreting the Score

  • 0-20%: Unrelated documents. Little to no overlap.
  • 20-40%: Some shared terms (likely same topic or domain).
  • 40-60%: Significant overlap possibly related drafts or a common source.
  • 60-80%: High overlap likely derivative (one built on the other).
  • 80-100%: Near-identical probably a copy or minor edit.
