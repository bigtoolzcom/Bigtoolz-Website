📚 What is Merge PDF Alternately?

Merge PDF alternately combines two PDFs by interleaving their pages taking page 1 from PDF A, page 1 from PDF B, page 2 from PDF A, page 2 from PDF B, and so on. The result is a single PDF that alternates between the two source files.

Unlike normal "merge" (which appends B's pages after A's), alternate merge creates a shuffle pattern. This is essential when the original pages have been scanned in two separate sets like odd and even pages, or English and Hindi versions of a document.

BigToolz runs this 100% in your browser using PDF.js and pdf-lib. Your PDFs never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🎯 When to Use Alternate Merge

📄 Dual-Sided Scans

If you scanned a book's odd pages into one PDF and even pages into another, alternate merge restores the original reading order: A1 (odd), B1 (even), A2, B2, ...

🌐 Bilingual Documents

For a bilingual book where each English page faces a Hindi translation, alternate merge creates the side-by-side reading pattern.

📊 Front + Back Cards

If you have all the "fronts" of cards in one file and "backs" in another, alternate merge pairs them correctly.

📋 Form + Answer Sheets

Forms in one PDF and their corresponding answers in another alternate merge pairs each form with its solution.

📸 Before + After Photos

Present before/after photos alternately for a compelling comparison document.

📐 The 3 Pattern Options

🔀 A → B (Default)

Standard interleave: A1, B1, A2, B2, A3, B3, ...

🔁 B → A

Same as A → B but starts with PDF B: B1, A1, B2, A2, ...

✏️ Custom Pattern

Enter a sequence like AAB, ABB, or AABB to control exactly how pages are taken:

  • AAB → A1, A2, B1, A3, A4, B2, ...
  • ABB → A1, B1, B2, A2, B3, B4, ...
  • AABB → A1, A2, B1, B2, A3, A4, B3, B4, ...

💡 Handling Different Page Counts

If PDF A has 10 pages and PDF B has 6, alternate merge produces:

A1, B1, A2, B2, A3, B3, A4, B4, A5, B5, A6, B6, A7, A8, A9, A10

The extra pages from the longer PDF are appended at the end in order. No pages are lost.
