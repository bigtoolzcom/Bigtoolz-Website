📅 What is a PDF Date Stamper?

A PDF date stamper adds a date (and optional prefix/suffix) to each page of your PDF. It's widely used for invoices, receipts, contracts, legal documents, and time-sensitive records to prove when a document was processed, received, or reviewed. Unlike handwritten dates or image watermarks, our tool writes real PDF text searchable, selectable, and copyable.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDFs never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, no file size limits.

🎨 What You Can Customize

Date Formats

  • YYYY-MM-DD (ISO 8601) Universal, sortable, unambiguous. Best for international and technical documents.
  • DD/MM/YYYY Common in Europe, India, and most of the world.
  • MM/DD/YYYY US convention. Avoid mixing with DD/MM format for clarity.
  • Long form "January 15, 2026" formal, elegant, best for contracts and certificates.

Prefix & Suffix

Add context to your date. Common examples: "Received:", "Approved on", "Reviewed:", "Invoice Date:". Suffix can include checkmarks (✓), emojis, or version markers.

Position

9 preset positions from the 3×3 grid. Bottom-left is most common for document tracking (like a received stamp). Top-right works well for header metadata. Center works for "VOID" style stamps.

Visual Style

  • Plain text subtle, elegant. Best for internal documents.
  • With background box solid color behind text, highlighted-style. Best for emphasis.
  • With border box rubber-stamp look. Best for formal, official documents.
  • Both background + border maximum visibility. For very important marks.

💡 Common Use Cases

  • Invoices & receipts stamp "Received on [date]" when payment is confirmed.
  • Contracts mark the effective date on every page for legal certainty.
  • Signed documents add "Approved [date]" after review.
  • Time-sensitive records timestamp documents for audit trails.
  • Batch processing stamp a batch of files with the same date.
  • Archival mark documents with processing or filing dates.
