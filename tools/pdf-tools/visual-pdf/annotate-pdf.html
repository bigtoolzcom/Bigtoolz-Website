💬 What is PDF Annotation?

PDF annotation adds comments, notes, or visual markers to a PDF without changing the original text. Common annotation types include text labels, sticky notes, rectangles, highlights, arrows, and freehand drawings. They're used for document review, collaboration, teaching, legal markup, and design feedback.

BigToolz uses pdf-lib to embed real annotations directly into the PDF file structure not as flattened images. Your annotations remain crisp at any zoom and appear in every PDF reader. Everything runs 100% in your browser no uploads, no signup.

🎨 The 3 Annotation Types

📝 Text Label

A rectangular text box with a colored left border. Best for review comments, section notes, corrections anything that needs to be readable in place. Text is drawn as a real vector string inside the PDF.

💬 Sticky Note

A small circular icon (like a comment marker) placed anywhere on the page. Best for short notes, references, and marginalia visually lightweight and doesn't cover the underlying text. The note content is stored as a text label next to the icon.

▭ Rectangle

A colored, semi-transparent box. Best for grouping elements, highlighting areas for review, callouts, and design feedback. Control the size and color to match your intent border, fill, or both.

📐 Understanding PDF Coordinates

PDF uses a bottom-left origin:

  • (0, 0) = bottom-left corner of the page
  • X increases going right
  • Y increases going up
  • Units are points 1 pt = 1/72 inch

Common Page Sizes

  • A4 595 × 842 pt
  • US Letter 612 × 792 pt
  • Legal 612 × 1008 pt
  • A3 842 × 1191 pt
  • A5 420 × 595 pt

Finding Coordinates

If you know the distance from the top of the page, use: Y = Page_Height − Distance_From_Top. For example, 3 inches (216 pt) from the top of a US Letter page → Y = 792 − 216 = 576.
