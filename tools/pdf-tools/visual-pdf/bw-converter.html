⚫ What is PDF to Black & White Conversion?

PDF to Black & White conversion removes color information from a PDF's pages and replaces it with either grayscale (256 shades of gray) or pure black & white (1-bit). This is useful for reducing file size, preparing documents for B&W printers, or standardizing the look of scanned documents.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy.

🎨 The 3 B&W Modes Explained

1. Grayscale (256 shades)

Converts every pixel to its luminance value 256 shades of gray from black to white. Best for photos and detailed images that need to retain visual information. File size reduction: typically 30-50% vs color.

2. High Contrast Grayscale

Grayscale with a contrast boost applied. Dark areas get darker, light areas get lighter makes text and line art pop. Best for text-heavy documents and mixed content. File size: similar to grayscale.

3. Pure Black & White (1-bit)

Each pixel becomes either fully black or fully white based on a threshold. Smallest file size (70-90% reduction) because each pixel takes just 1 bit. Best for text documents where photographic detail isn't critical. Photos will look posterized or lost.

💡 When to Use Each Mode

  • Scanned ID cards, receipts: Pure B&W text is crisp, file is tiny.
  • Books, ebooks: Pure B&W huge size savings, still readable.
  • Photo-heavy catalogs: Grayscale preserves visual quality.
  • Mixed reports: High Contrast text pops, images retained.
  • Legal documents: High Contrast crisp text + signature detail.
  • Presentation handouts (B&W printing): Grayscale at 2x DPI.
