⎁ What is PDF Underline Annotation?

Underline annotation draws a colored line below a specific range of text in a PDF. Unlike highlights (which overlay the text) or strikethroughs (which cross it out), an underline sits cleanly under the text perfect for emphasizing key terms, definitions, links, or important sentences without covering the original content.

📐 Understanding PDF Coordinates

PDF uses a bottom-left origin coordinate system. That means:

  • (0, 0) = bottom-left corner of the page.
  • X increases going right.
  • Y increases going up.
  • Units are points 1 pt = 1/72 inch = 0.353 mm.

Common Page Sizes (in points)

  • A4 595 × 842 pt
  • US Letter 612 × 792 pt
  • Legal 612 × 1008 pt
  • A3 842 × 1191 pt
  • A5 420 × 595 pt

Quick Conversion Formula

If you know the distance from the top of the page to where the underline should be, use:
Y = Page_Height − Distance_From_Top

For example, if you want an underline 3 inches (216 pt) from the top of a US Letter page: Y = 792 − 216 = 576 pt.
