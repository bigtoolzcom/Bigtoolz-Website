🖍 What is PDF Highlighting?

PDF highlighting adds a colored translucent overlay to text or regions of a PDF page. It's used for studying, document review, contract analysis, or calling attention to key information. Unlike annotations (which appear as separate comments), highlights visually integrate with the page content.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf.js and pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy.

🎯 The 2 Highlight Methods

1. Search & Highlight

Enter a word or phrase. Our tool finds all occurrences across every page and highlights each one in your chosen color. Perfect for:

  • Study material highlight all key terms (e.g., "definition", "important").
  • Contract review highlight every mention of a party's name or a specific clause.
  • Code documentation highlight all function names or parameters.
  • Research highlight specific data points across a paper.

2. Draw Regions

Click-drag on the preview canvas to draw a highlight rectangle over any area text, images, tables, or diagrams. Perfect for:

  • Highlighting images or charts that don't have selectable text.
  • Scanned PDFs where text isn't machine-readable.
  • Hand-drawn annotations mark up diagrams and figures.
  • Precise control highlight exactly what you want, nothing more.

🎨 Color Guide

  • Yellow (#facc15) the classic highlighter color. Best for general use.
  • Green (#4ade80) for "approved", "good", or "verified" content.
  • Blue (#60a5fa) for "info", "reference", or neutral callouts.
  • Pink (#f472b6) for "action items" or "attention".
  • Purple (#a78bfa) for "questions" or "uncertain" content.
  • Orange (#fb923c) for "warnings" or "review needed".

✨ Key Features

💡 Tips for Best Results

  • Start with 35% opacity text remains readable while the highlight is visible.
  • Use yellow for most cases it's the most readable and familiar.
  • Search is case-insensitive "IMPORTANT" and "important" both match.
  • Partial matches work searching "conf" will match "confidential", "confirm", etc.
  • For multiple colors, run the tool multiple times apply yellow first, save, re-upload, apply green, etc.
