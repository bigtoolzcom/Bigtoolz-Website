📉 What is PDF Compression?

PDF compression reduces the file size of a PDF without significantly affecting how it looks. This makes PDFs easier to email, faster to upload, and cheaper to store. It's essential when a PDF is too large to attach (usually above 10-25 MB) or when you need to send dozens of PDFs through email or a web form.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🎯 How Compression Works

PDFs contain two main types of content:

  • Text and vectors Usually stored efficiently. Compressing these doesn't save much (they're already small).
  • Images Often the bulk of a PDF's size. These are what compression targets.

Our tool re-encodes page images as optimized JPEG with your chosen quality setting. By lowering the DPI and quality slightly, file size can drop dramatically without visible changes at normal viewing sizes.

💡 Best Practices

  • Start with Medium it's the sweet spot for most documents.
  • Check the visual quality at 100% zoom on your screen before sending.
  • Text-heavy PDFs compress less they're already efficient.
  • Scanned PDFs compress the most because they're all images.
  • Keep the original always save your original PDF; compression is a lossy process.
  • Try grayscale toggle for scans removes color data, saves even more.
