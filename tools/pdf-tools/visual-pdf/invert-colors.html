🌗 What is PDF Color Inversion?

PDF color inversion flips the colors of every page black text on white background becomes white text on black background. This creates a dark-mode PDF that's easier on the eyes for night reading, reduces eye strain, and can save battery life on OLED screens.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

ℹ️ How It Works Because color inversion requires pixel-level processing, this tool rasterizes each page to an image and applies the invert, then rebuilds the PDF. The result is image-based text searchability is lost. This is perfect for night reading, but for text-searchable archives, keep the original PDF.

🎯 The 3 Invert Modes

🌗 Full Invert

The classic dark mode. Every color is flipped black becomes white, white becomes black, red becomes cyan, etc. Best for text-heavy documents, articles, and ebooks. Simple, fast, and predictable.

🧠 Smart Invert

Preserves the original colors of images (photos, logos, diagrams) while inverting the background and text. Best for documents with embedded images you want to keep natural brochures, magazines, illustrated guides. Use the threshold slider to control what counts as "background".

⚫ Grayscale Invert

First converts the page to grayscale, then inverts. Result: white text on pure black. Best for scanned documents, old papers, and cases where you want a clean, minimal reading experience without distracting colors.

💡 When to Use PDF Inversion

  • Night reading dark mode reduces eye strain in low light.
  • Long documents ebooks, manuals, contracts reading on screen for hours.
  • Power saving on OLED screens, black pixels use less power.
  • Accessibility some users find dark mode easier to read (especially with astigmatism).
  • Screen recordings dark PDFs look better in tutorials and presentations.
  • Projector presentations dark backgrounds work better in dark rooms.

📌 When NOT to Invert

  • Printing inverted PDFs consume enormous amounts of ink. Keep original for print.
  • Color-critical work CMYK proofs, brand materials, photography.
  • Text-searchable PDFs searchability is lost. Use the original or an app-level dark mode reader.
  • Before OCR inverted pages confuse OCR engines. Keep originals for text extraction.
