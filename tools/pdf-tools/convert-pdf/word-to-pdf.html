📕 What is Word to PDF Conversion?

Word to PDF conversion turns a Word document (DOCX, DOC, or TXT) into a portable PDF file. PDFs preserve the layout you see, work on any device, and can't be accidentally edited which makes them the standard format for official documents, contracts, invoices, resumes, and reports.

Our free Word to PDF converter runs 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and mammoth.js. Your files never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, no signup.

🎯 What's Supported

✅ Preserved Well

  • Headings H1, H2, H3 formatted distinctly (larger, bolder).
  • Paragraphs preserved as flowing text with proper wrapping.
  • Bold & Italic inline emphasis is preserved using PDF font variants.
  • Bullet and numbered lists converted with proper indentation.
  • Line breaks respected between paragraphs.

⚠️ Limited Support

  • Tables text is extracted but table structure is flattened.
  • Images inline images are not embedded.
  • Floating text boxes text is captured but positioning is lost.
  • Multi-column layouts flattened to single column.
  • Headers & footers not preserved from the Word file.
  • Page numbers not carried over.

Best Results With

Text-focused documents work best: essays, reports, letters, articles, notes, contracts, resumes without images. For documents with complex layouts and images, use Microsoft Word's built-in "Save as PDF" or "Export → PDF" feature.
