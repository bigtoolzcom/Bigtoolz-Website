🖼 What is PDF to PNG Conversion?

PDF to PNG conversion extracts each PDF page as a lossless PNG image. Unlike JPG, PNG preserves every pixel exactly no compression artifacts, perfect for text-heavy documents, diagrams, charts, screenshots, and line art. PNG also supports transparency, so you can remove the white background for logos and signatures.

Unlike most online PDF converters, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

Which Should You Use?

Choose PNG when quality matters most: documents with text, technical diagrams, screenshots, charts, logos, or any image that needs a transparent background. Choose JPG when file size matters for sharing photos or quick previews. Check our PDF to JPG tool for the smaller option.

📐 Scale Guide

  • 1x (72 DPI): Fast, small files. Best for quick previews or thumbnails.
  • 2x (144 DPI): Recommended for most uses sharp on screens, reasonable file size.
  • 3x (216 DPI): High-quality good for printing or zooming in on details.
  • 4x (288 DPI): Print quality larger files, but maximum sharpness for professional print work.
