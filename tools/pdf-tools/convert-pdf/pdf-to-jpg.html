🖼 What is PDF to JPG Conversion?

PDF to JPG conversion extracts the visual content of each PDF page and saves it as a separate image file. This is useful when you need to share individual PDF pages on social media, embed PDF pages in a website, send previews via email, or use PDF pages as images in other documents.

Unlike most online PDF converters, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

📐 Which Scale Should You Use?

  • 1x (72 DPI): Fast, small files. Best for quick previews or thumbnails.
  • 2x (144 DPI): Recommended for most uses sharp on screens, reasonable file size.
  • 3x (216 DPI): High-quality good for printing or zooming in on details.
  • 4x (288 DPI): Print quality larger files, but maximum sharpness for professional print work.
