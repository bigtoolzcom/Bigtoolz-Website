✂️ What is Split PDF?

Split PDF is a free online tool that divides a large PDF file into smaller PDF files. You can extract specific pages, split at regular intervals, or separate every single page into its own file. Splitting is lossless text, images, links, and formatting are preserved exactly as in the original.

Unlike most online PDF splitters, BigToolz runs 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🎯 The 3 Split Modes Explained

📄 Custom Ranges

Extract specific page ranges into separate PDFs. Ideal when you need only certain pages like chapters from a book, sections of a report, or specific invoices from a bulk document.

Examples:

  • 1-3,5,7-9 → 3 PDFs: pages 1-3, page 5, pages 7-9
  • 1-5 → 1 PDF: pages 1 through 5
  • 10-20 → 1 PDF: pages 10 through 20

🔢 Every N Pages

Automatically split the PDF at regular intervals. Perfect for splitting a 100-page document into 10 files of 10 pages each, or scanned batches into fixed-size chunks.

Examples:

  • 2 pages: A 10-page PDF → 5 files of 2 pages each
  • 10 pages: A 100-page PDF → 10 files of 10 pages each

✂️ Split All Pages

Convert each page into its own separate PDF. Ideal for:

  • Splitting scanned multi-invoice PDFs into individual invoices
  • Breaking up portfolios into single-page samples
  • Distributing a multi-page form as individual pages
  • Creating per-page files for sharing or archiving

💡 When to Split a PDF

  • Email attachments: Most email providers cap attachments at 25 MB. Splitting large PDFs makes them emailable.
  • Extracting content: Pull just the pages you need without sharing the full document.
  • Sharing chapters: Give each team member only their relevant section.
  • Reducing file size: Share smaller chunks instead of one big file.
  • Reorganizing: Split first, then re-merge in a different order.
  • Archiving: Store single pages as separate files for easier retrieval.
