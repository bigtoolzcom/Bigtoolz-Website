🔃 What is Reverse PDF?

Reverse PDF flips the page order of a PDF document the last page becomes the first, the first becomes the last, and everything in between swaps to the mirror position. It's a simple but powerful fix for scanned documents that were saved backwards, book scans with reversed pagination, and any PDF where page order got flipped accidentally.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results. The reversal only changes the page order the content of each page stays 100% identical.

🎯 Two Reversal Modes

Reverse All Pages

Flips the entire document. If your PDF has pages 1,2,3,4,5, the result becomes 5,4,3,2,1. Perfect for fixing a fully backwards document in one click.

Reverse Selected Only

Flips only the pages you select, keeping their positions in the document. If pages 2,4,6 are selected, they swap to become 6,4,2 but they stay in positions 2, 4, and 6 respectively. Great for mixing things up without affecting the rest of the document.

💡 When to Reverse a PDF

  • Scanned books saved backwards a common scanner bug.
  • Historical manuscripts some original right-to-left documents.
  • Accidental reversals undo a previous reverse operation.
  • Reading order fixes for documents meant to be read bottom-up.
  • Correcting two-sided scans when both sides of a page were scanned in reverse.
  • Comics and manga some scanlation groups need reversed page order.
