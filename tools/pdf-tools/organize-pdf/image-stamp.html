🖼 What is Image Stamp to PDF?

Image Stamp to PDF adds an image or logo directly onto the pages of a PDF as a visible overlay. Common uses include branding documents with a company logo, adding a signature, stamping "DRAFT" or "CONFIDENTIAL", watermarking with a logo, or adding a QR code to each page.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF and stamp image never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results. The stamp is embedded as an image overlay the original content is 100% preserved.

📐 Best Practices

Use PNG with Transparency

For logos and text stamps, always use PNG with transparent background. This way the stamp blends cleanly into the page without a visible white box behind it. JPG works too, but any solid-colored background will show up on the PDF.

Size Guide

  • 2-5%: Tiny stamp (QR codes, small logos).
  • 5-10%: Small subtle corner logo or signature.
  • 10-20%: Medium standard branding stamp.
  • 20-40%: Large prominent watermark or "CONFIDENTIAL" stamp.
  • 40-80%: Full-page watermark.

Opacity Guide

  • 20-40%: Subtle watermark (doesn't obstruct reading).
  • 50-70%: Balanced clearly visible but readable underneath.
  • 80-100%: Bold full-color stamp on top.

Rotation for Watermarks

Rotate the stamp to 45° for the classic diagonal watermark look. This makes it harder to crop or cover while still looking professional. Use 0° for a signature or a small corner logo.
