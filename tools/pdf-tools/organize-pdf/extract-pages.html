📄 What is Extract PDF Pages?

Extract PDF Pages is a free online tool that creates a brand-new PDF containing only the pages you select. Whether you need page 5 from a 100-page report, a cover page and chapter 3, or non-consecutive pages like 1, 7, and 42 this tool does it in seconds.

Unlike most online PDF tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results, no file size limits from servers.

📄 Page Range Examples

  • 1 → Only the first page
  • 1-5 → Pages 1 through 5
  • 1,3,5 → Pages 1, 3, and 5 only (non-consecutive)
  • 1-3,7,9-11 → A mix of ranges and single pages
  • 10-1 → Reversed range (extracts pages in reverse order)
