✂️ What is Crop PDF?

Crop PDF changes the visible area of each PDF page trimming blank margins, removing scanner artifacts, or cutting pages down to a specific size. It's essential when scanned documents have large white borders, when you need to fit content into a specific print size, or when you want clean edges for presentations.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results. Cropping modifies only the page's CropBox the content inside is 100% preserved.

📐 Three Crop Modes

1. Manual Margins

Precise control enter the exact amount to trim from each side (top, bottom, left, right) in millimeters. Best when you know the exact dimensions of the margins to remove, or need uniform trimming across all pages.

2. Auto-Crop Whitespace ✨

The smartest option for scanned documents. Each page is analyzed to find where the actual content starts and ends. Blank margins are automatically detected and trimmed. You control the threshold (how white counts as "blank") and extra padding (a safety buffer around content).

3. Crop to Preset Size

Crop every page to a standard paper size (A4, Letter, A5, etc.). Choose alignment (center, corners) and scale mode (crop without scaling, or scale down to fit). Perfect for standardizing mixed-size PDFs.
