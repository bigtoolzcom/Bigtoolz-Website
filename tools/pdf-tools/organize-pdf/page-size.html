📐 What is Change PDF Page Size?

Change PDF Page Size converts every page in a PDF to a different page dimension like switching a US Letter document to international A4, or resizing a tabloid report down to standard letter size. It's essential when you need consistent page sizes across a document, or when you're preparing a PDF for printing on specific paper.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results. Content never gets cropped it's scaled and centered automatically.

🎯 The 3 Scaling Modes

Fit (Recommended)

Scales the original content to fit within the new page while preserving aspect ratio. Content never gets distorted you may see small margins on one dimension if the aspect ratios differ. Best for most conversions.

Stretch

Fills the entire new page by scaling independently on each axis. Fills every pixel but may distort the aspect ratio. Useful when you want to eliminate all margins and don't mind slight visual stretching.

Center

Places the original content at its original scale, centered on the new page. Content may appear smaller (with margins) or larger (extending beyond the page will be clipped). Best for small size adjustments or adding margins.
