🔍 What is a PDF Duplicate Remover?

A PDF Duplicate Remover scans a PDF to find pages that look identical or nearly identical, then lets you remove the extra copies. It's essential for cleaning up scanned documents that accidentally got double-scanned, presentations with repeated slides, or merged PDFs with overlapping content.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz uses perceptual hashing each page is rendered as an image and reduced to a compact fingerprint. Two pages that look visually similar will have similar hashes, even if they're not byte-for-byte identical. Everything runs 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js.

🎯 The 3 Similarity Levels

🎯 Exact

Compares page pixel-by-pixel. Flags only pages that are truly identical (usually 95%+ identical). The safest setting no false positives.

🔒 Strict (Recommended)

Uses perceptual hashing with a tight threshold (95%). Catches pages that differ only in tiny details rescan of the same page, tiny anti-aliasing differences, or pages with slightly different compression. Best for scanned documents.

🌊 Loose

Uses a looser threshold (85%) to find visually similar pages like near-identical slides with minor text changes. Higher chance of false positives always review manually before removing.
