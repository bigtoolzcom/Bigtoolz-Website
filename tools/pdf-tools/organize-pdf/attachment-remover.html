📎 What are PDF Attachments?

PDF attachments (also called embedded files) are extra files bundled inside a PDF. They can be any file type Word documents, Excel sheets, images, ZIP archives, or even other PDFs. They're added to a PDF using tools like Adobe Acrobat's "Attach File" feature and can carry important supplemental material or hidden data you didn't know about.

Unlike most online tools, BigToolz processes everything 100% in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant results.

🔍 Why Remove PDF Attachments?

🔒 Privacy & Security

Attachments can contain hidden sensitive files old drafts, internal documents, or metadata you don't want to share. When you forward a PDF, you forward every attachment inside it. Removing them ensures a clean, safe document.

📉 Reduce File Size

Attachments can be huge. An embedded video, hi-res image, or ZIP file can add 10-100 MB to a PDF that should be 500 KB. Removing attachments can dramatically shrink the file.

📧 Email & Upload Limits

Many email servers reject attachments over 25 MB. Removing PDF attachments often brings a file under the limit, making it shareable again.

⚖️ Compliance & Legal

In legal, medical, or corporate settings, hidden attachments may violate data protection policies (GDPR, HIPAA, etc.). Cleaning them is often required before sharing.

🛡️ Malware Prevention

PDF attachments can hide malicious executables or scripts. Removing them protects recipients from potential threats.
