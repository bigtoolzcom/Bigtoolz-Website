📖 What is a PDF Reader?

A PDF Reader lets you open and read PDF files directly in your browser without installing Adobe Acrobat, Foxit, or any other application. Just drag & drop your PDF and it opens instantly with full navigation, zoom, search, and thumbnail preview capabilities.

Unlike most online PDF readers, BigToolz runs everything 100% in your browser using pdf.js the same engine Mozilla Firefox uses for its built-in PDF viewer. Your PDF never uploaded to any server complete privacy, instant rendering.

✨ Key Features

📄 Page Navigation

Move through the PDF with first, previous, next, and last page buttons. Or type a page number in the input to jump directly. The total page count always displays next to the input.

🔍 Zoom Controls

  • Zoom In / Zoom Out step through preset zoom levels (25% to 500%).
  • Fit to Width scales the page to fill the available width. Best for reading text-heavy documents.
  • Fit to Page scales the page to fit both width and height. Best for viewing images and layouts.

🔄 Rotation

Rotate the current view 90° left or right perfect for reading landscape pages in portrait orientation, or fixing sideways scans without modifying the original file.

🔎 Text Search

Search for any text within the PDF press the search icon or Ctrl+F. Matches are counted and navigable with Prev/Next buttons. Works on text-based PDFs (scanned PDFs need OCR first).

🖼 Thumbnails

Toggle the thumbnails strip to see mini previews of every page. Click any thumbnail to jump directly to that page. Great for navigating long documents or finding a specific section visually.

🖨 Print & Download

Print using your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P) or download the original file back to your device at any time.
