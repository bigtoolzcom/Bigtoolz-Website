⭐ What is a Favicon?

A favicon ("favorite icon") is the small square image that appears in browser tabs, bookmarks, history lists, and browser address bars. On mobile devices, favicons are used as app icons when users add your site to their home screen. A favicon is a small but essential part of brand identity and user experience it helps users quickly identify your site among many open tabs.

Modern websites need favicons in multiple sizes: 16×16 for browser tabs, 32×32 for retina displays, 180×180 for Apple devices, and 192×192 + 512×512 for Android/PWA. Our generator creates all of them from one image instantly.

🎯 Favicon Best Practices

Use a Square Image

Favicons are displayed square. If your logo is not square, it will be stretched or cropped. If you have a horizontal logo, use just the icon/mark portion.

Keep it Simple

At 16×16 pixels, there's no room for detail. Simple shapes, bold colors, and high contrast work best. Avoid text it becomes unreadable.

Use Transparency

Transparent PNGs look clean on all browser themes (light and dark). A solid background is safer for logos that rely on color, but transparency is more versatile.

Test Both Light and Dark Modes

Some browsers use dark backgrounds for tabs. A favicon with a white background will look out of place. Transparent or dark-colored icons work in both themes.

Start with 512×512+

Always start with the largest size (512×512 or 1024×1024) so downscaling produces crisp results. Upscaling from 32×32 will look blurry.
