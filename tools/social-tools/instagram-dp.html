📷 What is an Instagram DP Downloader?

An Instagram DP Downloader is a free online tool that extracts the profile picture (DP = Display Picture) of any Instagram account in the highest available quality. Since Instagram removed public API access in 2020, most online DP downloaders stopped working but our tool uses a reliable HTML source method that still works perfectly in 2026.

Whether you need someone's profile picture for reference, research, or personal use, our tool extracts it in HD quality (usually 320×320 pixels Instagram's highest public resolution).

🎯 Why Does This Method Work?

Instagram's public API was shut down in 2020 for privacy reasons. Third-party tools that used it stopped working. However, when you open Instagram in a browser while logged in, the page loads the profile picture in the HTML source. Our tool simply extracts the URL from that source.

This method is 100% safe because:

  • We never access your Instagram account you just paste HTML.
  • No login required on our end everything runs in your browser.
  • No data is uploaded to our servers parsing happens locally.
  • You stay logged in on your browser we don't touch your session.

📏 Instagram DP Resolutions

Instagram serves profile pictures in two resolutions:

  • Standard (150×150): Lower quality, older format
  • HD (320×320): Highest public resolution Instagram offers

Our tool extracts the HD version (320×320) when available that's the maximum Instagram provides. Higher resolutions are not accessible without Instagram Business API access.
