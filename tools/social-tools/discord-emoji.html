📐 Discord Emoji Requirements (2026)

Before you upload your custom emoji to Discord, here's what you need to know:

File Requirements

  • Size: 128 × 128 pixels (Discord's official size)
  • File Size: Under 256 KB per emoji
  • Format: PNG (static) or GIF (animated)
  • Transparency: PNG supports transparent backgrounds

Server Limits by Tier

  • Free Server: 50 static emojis, 50 stickers
  • Tier 1 Boost: 100 static, 30 animated slots
  • Tier 2 Boost: 150 static, 60 animated slots
  • Tier 3 Boost: 250 static, 120 animated slots

Who Can Upload

Only users with the "Manage Emojis" permission (usually admins/mods) can upload custom emojis. Server owners have this by default.

🎨 Emoji Design Tips

Keep It Simple

128×128 is tiny. Detailed designs become unreadable. Stick to 1-2 characters, or a bold icon. Simple shapes, thick lines, and high contrast win every time.

Use Bold Fonts

Thin fonts disappear at small sizes. Use bold weights (700-900) and chunky fonts like Arial Black or Impact.

High Contrast

Discord's background is dark (#313338). Use white text on colored backgrounds, or bright colors on transparent for maximum readability.

Match Your Server Theme

If your server uses a specific color scheme, match your emoji colors to it. This creates visual consistency across all custom emojis.

Keep a Consistent Style

If you're making multiple emojis, keep the font and style consistent. Users will start recognizing your emojis instantly in chat.
