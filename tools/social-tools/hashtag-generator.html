📊 Hashtag Strategy by Platform (2026)

📸 Instagram

3-5 hashtags per post is now optimal. Instagram's 2024 update penalizes hashtag stuffing. Focus on niche + relevant tags rather than broad ones. Mix 1-2 high-volume tags with 3-4 medium-competition tags.

♪ TikTok

3-5 hashtags. TikTok's algorithm prioritizes watch time and engagement over hashtags. Use 1-2 trending tags + 2-3 niche-specific tags. The "For You" page uses interest signals more than hashtags now.

𝕏 Twitter

1-2 hashtags max. Twitter/X's culture discourages heavy hashtag use. One strategic tag can boost discoverability. More looks spammy.

💼 LinkedIn

3-5 hashtags. LinkedIn's algorithm loves hashtags for professional content. Use industry + role hashtags. They help you reach beyond your network.

▶ YouTube

3-5 hashtags in the description. YouTube shows the first 3 tags above the video title. Choose wisely those 3 become clickable, visible tags.

⚠️ Common Hashtag Mistakes

  • Using 30 hashtags on Instagram. This used to be optimal; now it actively hurts reach.
  • Copying the same set on every post. Instagram flags repetitive hashtag use as spam.
  • Using banned hashtags. Some hashtags are flagged for inappropriate content using them shadowbans your post.
  • Only using generic hashtags like #love #instagood #photooftheday you're competing with millions of posts.
  • Ignoring niche tags. #coffeeloverdaily beats #coffee in engagement.
  • Hashtags in comments. There's no evidence they boost reach more than in captions just put them in the caption.
  • Not localizing. Adding city tags (#mumbai #delhi) increases local discoverability.

💡 Hashtag Strategy Tips

  • Research your competitors. See which hashtags top accounts in your niche use.
  • Use the 3-5-10 rule. 3 niche tags, 5 mid-size tags, 1-2 large-volume tags.
  • Mix evergreen + trending. Steady niche tags + current events.
  • Rotate your tags. Change them post-to-post to avoid algorithm penalties.
  • Track performance. Instagram Insights shows how many views came from hashtags use this data.
  • Create a branded hashtag. Like #YourBrandStory encourages UGC.
  • Check hashtag size. Under 500K posts = small/niche. 500K-5M = medium. 5M+ = broad.
