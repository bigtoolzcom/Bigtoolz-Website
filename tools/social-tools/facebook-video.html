⚖️ Is It Legal to Download Facebook Videos?

It depends on the content and how you use it.

  • Public videos for personal use: Generally OK Facebook's ToS allows you to save content for personal, non-commercial use.
  • Copyrighted content: If the video contains copyrighted music, film clips, or third-party content, downloading may violate copyright law.
  • Re-uploading: Downloading and re-uploading someone else's video to any platform (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram) without permission is copyright infringement.
  • Commercial use: Using downloaded Facebook videos in ads, monetized content, or business materials without a license is illegal.
  • Private videos: Downloading videos from private accounts or closed groups without permission is a violation of privacy and Facebook's ToS.

Best practice: Download only what you need for personal use, always credit the original creator if you share, and never monetize content that isn't yours.

💡 Facebook Video Download Tips

  • Use HD when available. Facebook stores videos in multiple resolutions. HD (1080p) gives the best viewing quality.
  • Check file size. A 5-minute HD video can be 100+ MB. SD is better for sharing via messaging apps.
  • Extract audio separately. If you only need the sound (music, speech), use a downloader that extracts MP3.
  • Use a VPN if blocked. Some regions block Facebook video downloads. A VPN can help.
  • Watch for fake tools. Many "downloaders" are ad farms that waste your time. Stick with trusted names like FDown, Getfvid.
  • Never enter your Facebook password. No legitimate downloader needs your login. If a site asks, leave immediately.
  • Reels and Stories are different. For Facebook Reels, use a dedicated Reels downloader.
