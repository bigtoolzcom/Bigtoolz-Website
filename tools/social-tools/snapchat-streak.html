👻 What is a Snapchat Streak?

A Snapchat streak is the count of consecutive days you and a friend have exchanged snaps. Each day both of you send at least one snap, the streak number increases by 1. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero, along with any record or bragging rights.

Streaks are one of the most addictive features on Snapchat. Some users maintain streaks for 1,000+ days (nearly 3 years), turning them into a daily ritual. Our Snapchat Streak Calculator helps you track exactly how long your streak has been going, what milestone you're approaching, and whether you need to snap right now to save it.

🔥 Snapchat Streak Emojis Explained

Snapchat uses different emojis next to streaks to show status:

  • 🔥 Fire You have an active streak with this friend. The number next to it is your total streak days.
  • ⌛ Hourglass Your streak is about to expire (usually within 4 hours). Send a snap NOW to save it.
  • 💯 Hundred You've hit 100 days. This is a major milestone that many users celebrate with a screenshot.
  • 📸 Camera You need to send a snap to start or maintain a streak.
  • ✨ Sparkles A new streak has just started (day 1 or 2).
  • ❄️ Snowflake You're on day 3-5 of the streak. A "cold" streak just warming up.

💡 How to Never Lose Your Snapchat Streak

  • Set a daily alarm. If you're forgetful, a 9 PM reminder can save a 500-day streak.
  • Use Snapchat's streak reminder. The app sends notifications when your streak is at risk.
  • Send quick snaps. A photo of the ceiling counts. Snap doesn't care about quality.
  • Both people must snap. If your friend misses, your streak breaks too even if you never missed.
  • Watch for the hourglass. When ⌛ appears, you have less than 24 hours (usually ~4 hours) to send.
  • Restore the streak. Snapchat lets you restore one broken streak per year for 24 hours but only once.
  • Don't delete the chat. Deleting the chat thread permanently resets the streak, even if both of you keep snapping.
