📏 Anatomy of a Great Telegram Bio

The 3-Element Formula

Given the tight 70-character limit, every Telegram bio should have 3 core elements:

  • Who you are Role, profession, or identity (e.g., "Developer", "Designer", "Student")
  • Where you're based City or country (builds connection)
  • How to reach you Telegram handle, email, or website

Examples That Work

  • 💻 Developer · Mumbai · @arjun 3 elements, under 30 chars
  • 📸 Photographer · Delhi · DM for shoots role + location + CTA
  • ✍️ Writer · Building in public · ↓ blog role + activity + link

💡 Telegram Bio Best Practices

  • Stay under 70 characters. Telegram's limit is 70 for regular accounts, 120 for Premium. Aim for 50-65.
  • Use 2-3 emojis. Emojis add personality without wasting much space.
  • Skip buzzwords. "Hustler", "Guru", "Visionary" overused and eye-roll-worthy.
  • Add a call to action. "DM me", "Say hi", "Link in bio".
  • Update regularly. Your bio should reflect what you're working on now.
  • Don't put a link. Telegram has a separate link field don't waste bio characters on URLs.
  • Use custom emoji if Premium. Telegram Premium users can use animated and custom emojis in bios.
